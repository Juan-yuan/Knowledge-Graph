Source
The whole plant of Water Bie (Shuǐbiē) of the Water Bie family.
[Original morphology]
Water Bie (Shuǐbiē) (Bencao Gangmu - Compendium of Materia Medica) is an aquatic floating herb with stolons and fibrous roots. Leaves thick, circular or reniform, about 2.5–6 cm long and about 2.5–6.5 cm wide, margin entire, dark green above, slightly reddish-purple below, and containing buoyant tissue, therefore able to float on the water surface; petiole rounded and stout, about 1–8 cm long; flowers unisexual, borne in the leaf axil; male flowers 2–3, clustered on the peduncle, with two leaf-like spathes; sepals 3; petals 3, white; stamens 9, filaments forked, anthers basifixed to the filaments; female flower solitary within the spathe, perianth equal in number to the male, with six sterile stamens arranged in pairs; ovary inferior, ovoid, 6-locular, styles 6, filiform, apex 2-cleft. After flowering it forms a globose-ovoid fleshy fruit containing many seeds.
[Habitat and distribution]
Commonly grows in ponds and marshes. Distributed in Hebei, extending south to East China, and Yunnan and other regions.
Properties and taste
Nature slightly cold, taste bitter and slightly salty.
Effects of Water Bie (Shuǐbiē)
Functions
The whole plant of Water Bie (Shuǐbiē). Mainly treats women's red and white vaginal discharge.
Indications
Clears heat and drains dampness. Treats women's red and white vaginal discharge.
Dosage and administration
Internal use: grind to a powder, 2–4 g.
Formulas of Water Bie (Shuǐbiē)
For treating women's metrorrhagia and vaginal discharge: Shuǐxuánfù (Shuǐxuánfù) powdered, eaten together with cooked water buffalo meat. (The properties and taste below are from Diannan Materia Medica)
