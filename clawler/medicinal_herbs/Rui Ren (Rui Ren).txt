Cultivation and processing of Nux Prinsepiae (ruiren)
Source
This item is the dried mature fruit kernel of Rosaceae plants Prinsepia nux (ruihe) or Dentate-leaved Prinsepia (chiye bianhemu). Harvest the ripe fruits in summer and autumn, remove the pulp, wash, and sun-dry.
Processing
Remove impurities, wash, and dry. Crush before use.
Properties and meridians entered
Taste and nature: sweet, slightly cold. Enters the Liver meridian.
Functions and indications
Disperses wind and clears heat, nourishes the Liver and brightens the eyes. Used for red swollen painful eyes, inflamed and eroded eyelid margins, dim vision and photophobia.
Description
This item is ovoid to nearly ovoid, slightly flattened, 7–10 mm long, 6–8 mm wide, 3–5 mm thick. Surface pale yellow-brown or dark brown, with distinct net-like grooves and occasional brownish pulp residue; apex pointed, sides slightly asymmetric. Hard in texture. Seed flat-ovoid, seed coat thin, light brown or reddish-brown, easily peeled; two cotyledons, milky white, oily. Slight odor, slightly bitter taste.
Effects of Nux Prinsepiae (ruiren)
Therapeutic actions
This item is the dried mature fruit kernel of Rosaceae plants Prinsepia nux (ruihe) or Dentate-leaved Prinsepia (chiye bianhemu). Used for red swollen painful eyes, inflamed and eroded eyelid margins, dim vision and photophobia.
Dosage and administration
Oral: decoct in water, 3–10 g.
Topical: appropriate amount, de-oil and grind into a paste for instillation into the eye; or decoct for washing.
