Cultivation and Preparation of Fresh Ginger (Shengjiang)
Source
Derived from the fresh rhizome of the ginger plant of the Zingiberaceae family. Harvested from October to December when stems and leaves turn yellow. Dig up the rhizomes and remove stems, leaves, and adventitious roots.
Properties and Taste
Acrid, slightly warm.
Processing
Remove impurities, wash clean, and cut into thick slices when used.
Description
Rhizome irregularly lobed, slightly flattened, with finger-like branches, 4–18 cm long, 1–3 cm thick. Surface yellow-brown or gray-brown, with nodes; branch tips bear stem scars or buds. Texture brittle, breaks with a short fracture; cross-section pale yellow; cortex with distinct concentric rings; vascular bundles scattered. Aromatic, with a characteristic pungent taste.
Actions of Fresh Ginger
Functions and Efficacy
Derived from the fresh rhizome of the ginger plant of the Zingiberaceae family.
Main indications: wind-cold common cold, vomiting, phlegm-fluid retention, wheezy cough, abdominal distention, diarrhea; antidote for Pinellia (Banxia), Arisaema (Tiannanxing), and poisons from fish, crabs, birds, and beasts.
Meridians Entered
Enters the Lung, Spleen, and Stomach meridians.
Indications
Releases the exterior, disperses cold, stops vomiting, transforms phlegm. Treats wind-cold common cold, vomiting, phlegm-fluid retention, wheezy cough, abdominal distention, diarrhea; antidote for Pinellia (Banxia), Arisaema (Tiannanxing), and poisons from fish, crabs, birds, and beasts.
1. Bencao: removes foul odors and restores mental clarity.
2. Belu: treats cold damage with headache and nasal congestion, cough with rebellious qi.
3. Tao Hongjing: enters the five zang, expels phlegm and descends qi, stops vomiting, eliminates wind-damp cold and heat.
4. Yaoxinglun: resolves phlegm and water accumulation and descends qi; both fresh and dry ginger treat cough and acute seasonal disease and stop vomiting without suppressing appetite. Fresh combined with Pinellia (Banxia) treats acute epigastric pain; if there is internal heat and inability to eat, pound and take the juice with honey. Its juice combined with apricot kernel and boiled makes a decoction that descends all types of constrained qi and fullness of chest and diaphragm and alternating cold-heat qi.
5. Qianjin·Dietary Therapy: induces sweating and removes foul odors above the diaphragm.
6. Shiliao Bencao: eliminates excessive heat, treats cramps and fullness of the heart; stops vomiting, relieves vexation and depression, and opens stomach qi.
7. Bencaoshiyi: juice detoxifies medicines, breaks blood stasis and harmonizes the middle, removes cold and phlegm, and stimulates appetite.
8. Zhenzhuang: benefits Spleen and Stomach, disperses wind-cold.
9. Yiyuan: warms the middle and removes dampness; treats toxicity of Magnolia (Houpo) and Pinellia (Banxia).
10. Riyong Bencao: treats cold damage, wind-cold, headache, and obstruction of the nine orifices; enters Lung to open appetite, removes cold from the abdomen, and resolves fetid filth; antidote for bacterial and fungal toxins.
11. Materia Medica: raw ginger disperses, cooked ginger harmonizes the middle; resolves poisoning from eating wild birds; juice used to treat red eyes; pounded juice with yellow gelatin boiled into a plaster for wind-damp pain.
12. Bencao Congxin: ginger juice opens phlegm, treats food-induced choking, rescues sudden collapse, treats foul body odor, and rubs frozen ears. Roasted ginger harmonizes the middle and stops vomiting.
13. Huiyue Yijing: roasted ginger treats stomach cold, diarrhea, and acid regurgitation.
14. Modern Practical Chinese Materia Medica: effective for intestinal hernia pain.
Dosage and Administration
Internal use: decoction, 3–10 g; or pound to a juice and take.
External use: appropriate amount, pound and apply as a poultice; or stir-fry and apply hot as a compress; or squeeze juice and rub on.
Precautions and Contraindications
Contraindicated for those with yin deficiency and internal heat.
1. Materia Medica: long-term ginger consumption accumulates heat and harms the eyes. Hemorrhoid patients who eat much ginger with alcohol may develop rapid exacerbation. Those with ulcers who eat much may develop malignant flesh.
2. Bencao Jingshu: prolonged use injures yin and damages the eyes; contraindicated in yin deficiency with internal heat, yin-deficiency cough with hemoptysis, external deficiency with heat sweating, spontaneous sweating and night sweating, viscera toxins with bleeding, vomiting due to heat, fiery abdominal pain; avoid similarly to Allium.
3. Suixi Ju Diet Manual: contraindicated in internal heat and yin deficiency, red eyes and throat disease, blood disorders and ulcer pain, vomiting and diarrhea with heat, summer-heat symptoms, severe asthmatic wheeze, and in pregnancy and convalescence or after smallpox.

For patients whose chest feels like choking but not wheezing, like vomiting but not vomiting, like belching but not belching, and a frustrated, helpless feeling in the heart: half a sheng of Pinellia Rhizome (Banxia), one sheng fresh ginger juice. Use the two with three sheng of water, boil the Pinellia to obtain two sheng, add ginger juice and boil to obtain one and a half sheng, cool slightly. Divide into four doses, take three doses daily and one dose at night, stop when resolved, continue after stopping if needed. (Jinkui Yaolue, Shengjiang Banxia Tang)
4. For cold-phlegm cough: two liang fresh ginger, one liang rock candy. Three bowls water, decoct to half a bowl, drink warm and slowly. (Bencaohuiyan)
5. For a thirty-year cough: one jin white honey, two jin fresh ginger (take the juice). Weigh the two ingredients in a copper cauldron to determine amounts; add the honey, then add ginger juice and simmer over low heat until ginger juice evaporates and only the measured amount of honey remains. Take one dose in the morning like a date-sized pill, hold in the mouth; three times daily. Avoid all other foods. (Qianjin Fang)

Dietary Therapy Recipes
1. Angelica sinensis (Danggui) and Fresh Ginger Mutton Soup
30 g Angelica sinensis (Danggui), 30 g fresh ginger, 500 g mutton. Wash Danggui and fresh ginger with water and slice into large pieces. Remove membranes from mutton, blanch in boiling water to remove blood, remove, cool and cut into strips about 5 cm long, 3 cm wide, 1 cm thick. In a clay pot add appropriate clear water, place mutton strips, then add Danggui and fresh ginger. Bring to a boil over high heat, skim scum, then simmer over low heat about 1.5 hours until mutton is tender. Eat meat and drink soup.
Efficacy: nourishes blood and warms the middle, regulates menstruation and relieves pain. Indicated for blood deficiency with cold and abdominal cold pain, postpartum cold abdominal pain in women, or cold-type dysmenorrhea.
2. Fresh Ginger Congee
8 g fresh ginger, two jujubes, 100 g rice, season with salt and sesame oil. Slice or finely shred 8 g fresh ginger, two jujubes (for wind-cold common cold omit jujubes and use two scallion whites), 100 g rice, cook together with broth into congee, season with salt and sesame oil.
Contraindications: avoid in heat syndromes.
Efficacy: for headache and nasal congestion, chronic bronchitis, susceptibility to colds, obesity, Spleen and Stomach yang deficiency, stomach pain, etc.
