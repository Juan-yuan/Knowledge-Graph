Cultivation and Processing of Fistular Onion Stalk (Congbai)
Source
This item is the Welsh onion (Cong, Allium fistulosum L.) of the Liliaceae family; its bulb or whole herb is used medicinally. The whole herb can be harvested year-round, washed and used fresh; when using the white stalk (bulb) the outer membrane should be peeled off and the fibrous roots and leaves removed.
Nature and Taste
Acrid (pungent), warm.
Characteristics
Bulbs cylindrical, slightly enlarged at the apex, scaly leaves layered, white, with white longitudinal stripes. Leaves basal, cylindrical, hollow, about 45 cm long, 1.5–2 cm in diameter, apex pointed, green, with longitudinal veins; leaf sheaths pale green.
Effects of Fistular Onion Stalk (Congbai)
Efficacy
This item is the Welsh onion (Cong), a plant of the Liliaceae family; its bulb or whole herb is used medicinally.
Indications
Treats cold-damage with alternating cold and heat and headache, yin-type cold abdominal pain, internal obstruction due to parasite accumulation, difficulty or no passage of the two orifices (urination and defecation), dysentery, and swellings/abscesses (carbuncles).

Indications
Induces the exterior, unblocks yang, and detoxifies. Treats cold-damage with alternating cold and heat and headache; yin-type cold abdominal pain; internal obstruction from parasite accumulation; difficulty with urination and defecation; dysentery; swellings and abscesses.
1. Benjing: Mainly for cold-damage with alternating cold and heat; induces sweating, wind-struck conditions, and facial swelling.
2. Bielu: Treats cold-damage bone and muscle pain, throat obstruction, and calms the fetus (prevents miscarriage).
3. Meng Shen: Unblocks joints, stops epistaxis (nosebleed), and promotes urination and defecation.
4. Rihuazi Bencao: Treats epidemic febrile diseases, headache with fever and mania, opens the large and small intestines, treats cholera with convulsions and "zetun" qi, beriberi, epigastric and abdominal pain, dizziness, and relieves palpitations and mental confusion.
5. Yongyao Xinfa: Unblocks yang qi and disperses exterior wind pathogens.
6. Li Gao: Treats Yangming diarrhea with bloody stools.
7. Riyong Bencao: Can reach both exterior and interior, harmonize them, calm the fetus and stop bleeding.
8. Bencao Mengquan: For snake bites and earthworm bites, apply with a saline compress to resolve.
9. Compendium of Materia Medica: Expels wind-damp, relieves generalized body pain and numbness, treats parasite accumulation causing cardiac pain, stops adult yang collapse, treats yin-toxic abdominal pain, removes intestinal hookworms in infants, treats bleeding during pregnancy, promotes milk flow and disperses breast abscesses, relieves tinnitus, and is applied for rabid dog bite poison.
Dosage and Administration
Internal use: decoction, 9–15 g; or decocted in wine. When cooked into congee, use fresh product 15–30 g each time.
External use: appropriate amount, pounded and applied topically, stir-fried and used as a hot compress, decocted and used as a wash; mixed with honey or vinegar and applied.
Precautions and Contraindications
Those with exterior deficiency and excessive sweating should not take.
1. Qianjin·Dietary Therapy: Eating raw scallions and then eating honey produces diarrhea.
2. Dietary Materia Medica: It ascends and may attack the person, potentially causing collapse of the five viscera. Weak persons with qi disorders—excessive consumption provokes excessive qi.
3. Lvchanyan Materia Medica: Long-term consumption causes forgetfulness and especially aggravates chronic diseases. People with bromhidrosis should not eat.
4. Compendium of Materia Medica: When taking Rehmannia root (Dihuang) or Dichroa (Changshan), avoid eating scallions.
5. Ben Cao Jing Shu: Patients with exterior deficiency prone to sweating should not eat it; if the patient has already sweated, do not take more.

Divide into three doses and administer orally. (Hua Tuo Weibing Fang)
(5) For stomach pain, excessive stomach acid, and indigestion: four large scallion bulbs, four liang of brown sugar. Pound the bulbs, mix with brown sugar, place on a plate and steam until cooked. Take three times daily, three qian each time. (Inner Mongolia "Selected New Medical Methods of Chinese Herbs")
(6) For acute urgent pain from parasitic accumulation with clenched jaw and near collapse: five old fistular onion stalks (Congbai). (Ruizhu Tang Experience Formulas)
(7) For a newborn who does not urinate: four he of human breast milk and one cun of Fistular onion stalk (Congbai). Mix and decoct the two, divide into four doses. (Waitai Miyao Fang)
(8) For difficulty in urination and small intestine distention: three jin of Fistular onion stalk (Congbai). Finely scrape, stir-fry until hot, wrap in a cloth, divide into two pieces, and apply as a hot compress below the navel. (Benshi Fang)
(9) For infantile collapse: three stalks of Fistular onion stalk (Congbai). Decoction, mix with raw honey and powdered donkey-hide gelatin (Ejiao) for oral administration. Also soak the onion head in honey and insert into the anus. (Quanyu Xinjian)
(10) For cholera with restlessness and inability to lie still: twenty stalks of Fistular onion stalk (Congbai) and twenty Chinese red dates (Dazao). Water three sheng, boil down to two sheng, take at once. (Buque Zhouhou Fang)
(11) For Shaoyin disease with diarrhea: four stalks of Fistular onion stalk (Congbai), dried ginger (Ganjiang) one liang, aconite (Fuzi) one piece (raw, peel removed, split into eight pieces). Dietary Therapy Recipes
Scallion and Red Date Decoction
Twenty large red dates (Dazao) and seven Fistular onion stalks (Congbai). Wash the red dates and soak in water; wash the Fistular onion stalks (with roots) and set aside. Put the red dates into an aluminum pot, add an appropriate amount of water, bring to a vigorous boil over high heat, after about twenty minutes add the Fistular onion stalks and continue to simmer over low heat for ten minutes until done.
Administration: Eat the dates and drink the soup, take once in the morning and once in the evening.
Efficacy: Calms the mind and benefits heart qi. Indications: for heart qi deficiency manifesting as neurasthenia, insomnia with many dreams, and declining memory, etc.
