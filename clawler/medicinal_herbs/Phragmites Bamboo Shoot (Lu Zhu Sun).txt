Name
Giant reed shoot (Lúzhúsǔn)
Cultivation and processing of giant reed shoot (Lúzhúsǔn)
Source
The young shoots of the Poaceae plant giant reed (Lúzhú). Harvested in spring, washed clean and used fresh.
[Original morphology]
Giant reed (Lúzhú) is a perennial herb. It has a rhizome and stout fibrous roots. Culms erect, 2–6 m high, 1–1.5 cm in diameter, often branched. Leaf sheaths longer than the internodes, glabrous or with long soft hairs on the collar; ligules membranous, truncate, about 1.5 mm long, apex with short fine hairs; leaf blades flat, 30–60 cm long, 2–5 cm wide, surface and margins slightly rough when young. Panicle rather compact, 30–60 cm long, branches dense and ascending obliquely, spikelets contain 2–4 florets; glumes lanceolate, 8–10 mm long, with 3–5 veins; lemmas also with 3–5 veins, the midvein extending into a short awn 1–2 mm long, the lower part of the back densely covered with white soft hairs slightly shorter than the lemma, basal disk about 0.5 mm long, inner glume about half the length of the outer glume. Flowering period October–December.
[Habitat and distribution]
Ecological environment: grows beside streams and beside houses in relatively moist, deep soils.
Resource distribution: distributed in southwestern China, southern China and in Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Hunan and other areas.
Nature and taste
Chongqing Materia Medica: bitter in taste, cold in nature, non-toxic.
Effects of giant reed shoot (Lúzhúsǔn)
Functions
The young shoots of the Poaceae plant giant reed (Lúzhú). Mainly indicated for lung-heat hemoptysis, steaming-bone tidal fever, dizziness, hot lin (hot strangury), ear obstruction, toothache.
Main indications
Clears heat and drains fire. Treats lung-heat hemoptysis, steaming-bone tidal fever, dizziness, hot lin (hot strangury), ear obstruction, toothache.
1. "Categorized Herbal Properties": shoot juice treats ear pain.
2. "Lingnan Materia Medica": mashed young shoots can draw out rotten bone.
3. "Chongqing Materia Medica": clears fire and relieves heat, suitable for various febrile conditions. Treats steaming-bone tidal fever, fire-related toothache, dizziness, hot lin, etc.
Usage and dosage
Internal use: decoct in water, fresh product 15–60 g; or mash to extract juice, or boil down to a paste.
External use: appropriate amount, mash to extract juice and drop into the ear.
