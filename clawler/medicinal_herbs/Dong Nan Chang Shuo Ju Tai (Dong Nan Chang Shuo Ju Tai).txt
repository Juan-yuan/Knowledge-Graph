Name
Southeast long-fruited Lactuca (Dongnan changshuo jutai), Shima po zi grass (Shimapozicao)

Cultivation and processing of Southeast long-fruited Lactuca (Dongnan changshuo jutai)
Source
Whole herb of the plant of the family Lactucaceae, Southeast long-fruited Lactuca (Dongnan changshuo jutai). Harvested in spring and sun-dried.
Morphology
Southeast long-fruited Lactuca (Dongnan changshuo jutai) is a perennial herb. Rhizome cylindrical, about 4 cm long. Leaves 4–16, all basal; petioles 1.8–8 cm long, stout, with short coarse hairs; leaf blades papery, oblong or oblong-elliptic, 2.2–10 cm long, 1–3.6 cm wide, apex acute or slightly acute, base cuneate, margins with dense small teeth, both surfaces with short appressed hairs, lateral veins 5–7 per side. Inflorescences cymose-umbellate, 2–4, 2–3 times branched, with short appressed hairs; calyx 4.5–7 mm long, 5-lobed to the base, lobes narrow linear, abaxial surface with short appressed hairs, adaxial surface glabrous; corolla 1.5–2 cm long, abaxial surface with short soft hairs, adaxial surface nearly glabrous, narrowly campanulate, upper lip 3–5 mm long, 2-lobed to the middle, lobes obliquely flattened triangular, lower lip 3-lobed to the middle, lobes ovate; stamens glabrous, anthers elliptic, 2 staminodes degenerate, about 0.5 mm long; disc ring-shaped; pistil about 1.6 cm long, sparsely with small glands; ovary sessile, stigma flattened globose. Achene (cypsela) linear, 2–3.4 cm long, glabrous. Seeds narrowly elliptic or fusiform, 0.4–0.5 mm long. Flowering around April.
Habitat and distribution
Ecology: grows under forest in mountain valleys or on stones on mountain slopes or on rock cliffs.
Distribution: occurs in Jiangxi, Fujian, Hunan, Guangdong and other areas.
Properties and taste
Taste bitter; pungent; nature cold
Effects of Southeast long-fruited Lactuca (Dongnan changshuo jutai)
Efficacy
Whole herb of the plant of the family Lactucaceae, Southeast long-fruited Lactuca (Dongnan changshuo jutai). Mainly used for wind-heat attack, nasal congestion with nasal discharge, sneezing, cough.
Indications
Disperses wind-heat and relieves toxicity. Indicated for wind-heat invasion, nasal congestion with nasal discharge, sneezing, cough.
Usage and dosage
Internal use: decoction, 6–9 g.
