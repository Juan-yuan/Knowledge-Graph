Cultivation and processing of Sheng Teng (sheng teng)
Source
It is the stem-vine of Xuyaoteng (xu yao teng), a dicotyledonous medicinal plant of the Convolvulaceae family. Collected in summer and autumn. Sliced and sun-dried.
Properties and flavor
Sweet and pungent; warm
Effects of Sheng Teng (sheng teng)
Efficacy
It is the stem-vine of Xuyaoteng (xu yao teng), a dicotyledonous medicinal plant of the Convolvulaceae family. Mainly used for common cold, bronchitis, stomach pain, epigastric distension, and rheumatic pain.
Meridians
Spleen; Stomach meridians
Indications
Dispels wind and disperses cold; promotes qi movement and unblocks the collaterals. Indicated for common cold, bronchitis, stomach pain, epigastric distension, and rheumatic pain.
Usage and dosage
Internal use: decoction, 6-15 g; or grind into powder.
