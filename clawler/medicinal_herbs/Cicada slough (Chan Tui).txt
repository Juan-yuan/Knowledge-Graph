Cultivation and processing of Cicada Slough
Source
This material is the shed exoskeleton of the larvae of the cicada Cryptotympana pustulata Fabricius (family Cicadidae) after molting. Collected in summer and autumn, remove dirt and sand, sun-dry.
Properties (taste and nature)
Flavor: sweet; salty; Nature: cold
Processing
Boji Fang: remove earth, claws, face; wash clean and use.
Xiao Er Yao Zheng Zhi Jue: remove head, feet, and dirt; wash with water, sun-dry, grind to powder, use with water flying three times.
Paozhi Dafa: wash with boiling water to remove soil, remove head, wings and feet and use. Current practice: take the raw material, remove impurities, wash clean, dry.
Characteristics
Whole form resembles a cicada and is hollow, slightly curved. Length about 3–4 centimeters, width about 1.5–2 centimeters. Surface is tea-brown, semi-translucent, glossy, covered with fine black-brown or yellow-brown hairs. Head has one pair of antennae, filiform, often broken off; compound eyes protruding, transparent; forehead protruding; labrum short and broad, labium elongate into a tube. Dorsal thorax longitudinally split or split crosswise in a cruciform manner; left and right each have two pairs of small wings, the anterior pair longer, the posterior pair shorter; ventrally three pairs of legs, foreleg femur and tibia with serrations at the tips, foreleg tarsi with two small spines at tips, teeth and spines are black-brown; midlegs and hindlegs are slender and long. Abdomen flattened and rounded; divided into nine segments, tail end triangular and bluntly pointed. Body light, membranous, hollow, brittle. Smell weak, taste mild. The best are those yellow in color, light in weight, intact, and free of dirt and sand.
Effects of Cicada Slough
Functions
This material is the shed exoskeleton of the larvae of the cicada species Cryptotympana pustulata.
Main indications: external-contracted wind-heat, cough with hoarseness, measles failing to surface, urticarial itching, pediatric convulsions, eye redness, eye opacities, carbuncles and toxic swellings, tetanus.
Meridians
Lung meridian; Liver meridian
Indications
Disperse wind-heat, release the lungs, and stop spasms. Treats externally contracted wind-heat, cough with hoarseness, measles failing to surface, urticarial itching, pediatric convulsions, eye redness, eye opacities, carbuncles and toxic swellings, tetanus.
① Bie Lu: treats pediatric convulsions; ashes applied cure chronic dysentery.
② Yao Xing Lun: treats children with whole-body feverish convulsions and also quenches thirst.
③ Ben Cao Shi Yi: grind one qian spoon, take with well water, treats aphonia.
④ Ben Cao Yan Yi: treats blurred vision and eye opacities. Also decoct the shell to obtain juice, treats children with delayed eruption of rashes and discomfort.
⑤ Ben Cao Gang Mu: treats wind in the head causing vertigo, skin wind-heat, itchy smallpox eruptions, tetanus and treatment of swelling-toxin sores, adult loss of voice, pediatric trismus and upward-gazing spasms, fright-induced crying and night crying, genital swellings.
Dosage and administration
Internal use: decoct as a decoction, 3–6 g; or enter into pills or powders.
External use: appropriate amount, decocted water for washing; or grind to powder and apply as a paste.
Precautions and contraindications
Use with caution during pregnancy.
Formulas containing Cicada Slough
① For early-stage wind-warmth, newly contracted wind-heat, winter-warm invading the lung, cough: Mint (Bohe) 1 qian 5 fen, Cicada Slough (Chantui) 1 qian (remove feet and wings), Peucedanum Root (Qianhu) 1 qian 5 fen, Fermented Soybeans (Dandouchi) 4 qian, Trichosanthes Peel (Gualoupi) 2 qian, Burdock Seed (Niubangzi) 1 qian 5 fen. Decoction and take. (Shi Bing Lun, method of acrid-cool release of the exterior)
② For cough with obstructed lung qi: Cicada shell (Chanke, remove earth, lightly stir-fry), Ginseng (Renshen, remove fibrous root), Schisandra (Wuweizi) one liang each, Tangerine Peel (Chenpi), Licorice (Gancao, honey-fried) each half liang. Grind to fine powder. Each dose half qian, take with fresh ginger soup, no fixed time. (Xiao Er Wei Sheng Zong Wei Lun Fang, Cicada Shell Decoction)
③ For common cold with cough and loss of voice: Cicada garment (Chanyi) 1 qian, Burdock Seed (Niubangzi) 3 qian, Licorice (Gancao) 1 qian, Platycodon Root (Jiegeng) 1 qian 5 fen. Decoction and take. (Modern Practical Chinese Medicine)
④ For incompletely erupting smallpox: Lithospermum Root (Zicao), Cicada Slough (Chantui), Akebia Stem (Mutong), Peony (Bai Shao), Licorice (Gancao, honey-fried) equal parts. Each dose 2 qian, decoct and take. (Xiao Er Dou Zhen Fang Lun, Kuai Tou San)
⑤ For wind-invaded skin causing persistent itching: Cicada Slough (Chantui) and Mint leaves (Bohe Ye) equal parts. Grind to powder. Mix with wine to 1 qian spoon, take three times daily. (Yao Seng Tan Ji Yan Fang)
⑥ For post-smallpox fever and itching with scratching-induced lesions: Cicada retreat (Chantui) and Lycii Cortex (Digupi) each 1 liang. Grind to powder. Each dose 2–3 spoonfuls, take with white wine two to three times. (Chi Shui Xuan Zhu, Chantui Powder)
⑦ For convulsions with high fever and spasms: Cicada shell (Chanke, remove soil, stir-fry) 0.5 liang, Ginseng (Renshen, remove fibrous root) 0.5 liang, Scutellaria (Huangqin) 1 fen, Poria (Fushen) 1 fen, Cimicifuga (Shengma) 1 fen, grind the above to fine powder; Bovine gallstone (Niuhuang) 1 fen (male ground), Bamboo bezoar (Tianzhu Huang) 1 qian (ground), Oyster shell (Muli) 1 fen (ground). Mix evenly and finely; each dose half qian, decoct with Schizonepeta (Jingjie) and Mint (Bohe) soup and take as needed. (Xiao Er Wei Sheng Zong Wei Lun Fang, Cicada Shell Powder)
⑧ For pediatric upward-gazing spasm, head and eyes turned upward, phlegm blocking internal heat: Jinniuer, boil in slurry water for one day, sun-dry and grind to powder, each dose one character (a traditional small measure), take with cold water. (Wei Sheng Yi Jian Fang)
⑨ For pediatric trismus at birth with inability to suckle: Cicada Slough 27 pieces, Whole Scorpion (Quan Xie) 27 pieces. Grind to powder, add a small amount of Litargirio (Qingfen) powder, mix with breast milk and feed. (Quan You Xin Jian)
⑩ For pediatric night crying: Cicada retreat 27 pieces, Cinnabar (Zhusha) a small amount. Grind to powder, make honey pills, let the child suck. (Chi Shui Xuan Zhu, Chantui Paste)
(11) For tetanus: Cicada Slough (remove earth) any amount. Grind to fine powder. Sprinkle into the wound; the toxic qi will disperse. (Yang family household prescriptions, Zhui Feng Powder)
(12) For scrofulous sores affecting the eye or post-illness eye opacities: Cicada Slough (wash clean, remove earth) and White Chrysanthemum Flower (Juhua) equal parts. Each dose 2 qian, one cup of water, add a little honey, decoct and take after milk feeding, adjust dose by child size. (Xiao Er Dou Zhen Fang Lun, Cicada-Chrysanthemum Powder)
(13) For internal eye opacities: Dragon shed (that is Snake Slough), Cicada retreat, Fenghuang retreat (that is eggshell of ornamental fowl), Human shed (Ren Tui), Buddha slough (that is silkworm shed). Equal parts, do not measure quantitatively, burn together to ash, grind to fine powder. Each dose 1 qian, eat with hot pig liver, any time, three times daily. (Ophthalmology Long Mu Lun, Five Retreats Powder)
(14) For treating sores: Cicada retreat shell and White Stiff Silkworm (Baijiangcan) equal parts. Grind to powder, mix with vinegar and apply around the lesion, leaving the ulcer opening, wait until the root emerges somewhat long, then extract the root and then apply medicine to the sore. Another formula uses oil instead of vinegar for mixing and applying. (Sheng Hui Fang, Cicada Slough Powder)
(15) For scrofula: open a walnut, remove half the kernel, fill the cavity with Cicada Slough, seal externally with yellow loam, tie with iron wire, roast over low heat until dry and the mud falls off, then grind the walnut finely, take with yellow wine as an adjuvant, mix with hot water and drink, take one walnut on an empty stomach each morning, continue for one hundred days. (Hebei Traditional Chinese Medicine Collection)
(16) For otitis with pus discharge: Cicada Slough half a liang (heat-treated to retain activity), Musk (Shexiang) half qian (stir-fried), grind to powder, wrap in cotton and plug, expel the foul matter. (Hai Shang Fang)
(17) For pediatric genital swelling: Cicada Slough half a liang, decoct to wash; also give Wuling Powder internally, then the swelling subsides and pain stops. (Shi Yi De Xiao Fang)
Dietary therapy formula
Mulberry-Chrysanthemum-Cicada Slough Drink (Sang Ju Chantui Yin)
Mulberry Leaf (Sangye) 10 g, Chrysanthemum Flower (Juhua) 10 g, Cicada Garment (Chanyi) 6 g, White Sugar appropriate amount. Select and clean mulberry leaves and chrysanthemum flowers, remove head and feet of cicada garment, place all into a pot, add appropriate amount of water, bring to a boil over high heat, then simmer over low heat for 15 minutes, strain to obtain decoction. Add white sugar and stir to dissolve. Drink regularly.
Functions: dispel wind and clear heat, calm convulsions. Indications: pediatric convulsions with fever, headache, cough, runny nose, etc.
