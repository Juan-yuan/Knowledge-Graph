Cultivation and Processing of Xiangru
Source
From the whole plant with roots or the aerial parts of Lamiaceae plants Jiang Xiangru (Jiang Xiangru) or Hua Qizhu (Hua Qizhu). Harvest when stems and leaves are luxuriant and flowers begin to open in summer and autumn, cut and shade-dry or sun-dry, and tie into small bundles.
Properties and Flavor
Taste acrid; nature slightly warm
Preparation
Pick out impurities, spray with water to moisten, remove remaining roots, cut into sections, sun-dry to obtain.
Description
This product is 30–50 cm long, base purplish red, upper part yellow-green or pale yellow, whole plant densely covered with white puberulent hairs. Stem quadrangular and cylindrical; diameter 1–2 mm, nodes distinct, internodes 4–7 cm long; brittle, breaks easily. Leaves opposite, often curled or deciduous; when leaf blades are flattened they are oblong-ovate or lanceolate, dark green or yellow-green, margins with sparse serrations. Spike inflorescences terminal and axillary, bracts broadly ovate, caducous or persistent; calyx persistent, campanulate, pale purplish red or gray-green, apex 5-lobed, densely pubescent. Nutlets 4, nearly globose, reticulate, meshes depressed forming shallow pits. Aroma clear and strong, taste slightly acrid and cool.
Effects of Xiangru
Functions
From the whole plant with roots or the aerial parts of Lamiaceae plants Jiang Xiangru (Jiang Xiangru) or Hua Qizhu (Hua Qizhu).
Main Indications
Treats cold-damp accumulation from summer, headache, fever, aversion to cold without sweating, chest fullness and epigastric and abdominal pain, vomiting and diarrhea, edema, beriberi.
Channels Entered
Lung channel, Stomach channel
Indications
Induces sweating and relieves summer heat, promotes urination and disperses dampness, warms the stomach and harmonizes the middle burner. Treats cold-damp accumulation in summer, headache, fever, aversion to cold without sweating, chest fullness and abdominal pain, vomiting and diarrhea, edema, beriberi.
① "Bielu": Treats cholera, abdominal pain with vomiting and diarrhea, disperses edema.
② Meng Shen: Dispels hot wind, sudden convulsions, can boil the juice and take it in one gulp. Also dried powder stops nasal bleeding, taken with water.
③ "Rihuazi Materia Medica": Descends qi, removes vexing heat, treats vomiting and rebellious cold qi.
④ "Luchanyan Materia Medica": Arrests seasonal cold damage.
⑤ "Ben Cao Yan Yi Supplement": Treats summer injury, promotes urination.
⑥ "Dian Nan Materia Medica": Releases the exterior and expels pathogenic factors, treats summer heat headache, summer diarrhea and intestinal pain, summer-heat cough, induces sweating, warms the stomach, harmonizes the middle.
⑦ Wang Ying "Food Materia Medica": Boiled as a beverage instead of tea in summer, can prevent febrile disease, harmonize the middle and warm the stomach; hold the juice and gargle to remove bad breath.
⑧ "Compendium": Treats beriberi cold and heat.
Dosage and Administration
Internal use: decoction, 3–9 g; or used in pills or powders, or decocted and used as a gargle.
External use: appropriate amount, pounded and applied.
Precautions and Contraindications
Contraindicated for those with exterior deficiency.
1. "Ben Cao Cong Xin": Do not use when there is no external pathogenic factor.
2. "De Pei Materia Medica": Contraindicated for those with vigorous fire and qi deficiency, or yin deficiency with heat.
Formulas of Xiangru
① For disharmony of spleen and stomach, oppression and fullness of the chest and diaphragm, internal contraction by wind-cold, external invasion of cold pathogenic factors, aversion to cold with fever, body aches and limb fatigue, cholera vomiting, spleen pain with inverted stomach, drunkenness unaroused, seasonal cold headache: Xiangru (stolized soil removed) 2 liang, Licorice (Gancao) (honey-fried) 0.5 liang, White Hyacinth Bean (Bai Bian Dou) (stir-fried), Magnolia Bark (Houpo) (peeled, ginger-juice fried), Poria Shen (Fushen) each 1 liang. Grind into fine powder. Each dose 2 qian, pour boiling soup in and take with salt. (Jufang Xiangru Decoction)
② For cholera with vomiting and diarrhea, limb vexation and pain, cold sweat, great thirst: Xiangru 2 liang, Liaozi 1 liang. Coarsely pound and sieve the two ingredients. Each dose 2 qian, water one cup, decoct to seven-tenths, remove dregs and take warm, three times daily. (Shengji Zong Lu Xiangru Decoction)
③ For cholera abdominal pain, vomiting and dysentery: fresh Xiangru (cut) 1 sheng, small garlic 1 sheng (crushed), Magnolia Bark (Houpo) 6 liang (roasted), Fresh Ginger (Shengjiang) 10 liang. Cut the four ingredients, use one dou of water, boil to obtain 3 sheng, divide into three doses; causes vomiting and dysentery to stop, each dose must be warm. (Emergency Formulas Xiangru Decoction)
④ For sudden edema from water-retention, or water in sores, whole-body swelling: dry Xiangru 1 jin, Atractylodes Rhizome (Baizhu) 7 liang. Pound the two and sift; concentrate boil Xiangru to obtain juice, mix with Baizhu to form pills the size of a parasitic seed. Each dose 10 pills, take four to five times day and night, very effective to promote urination. In summer using flowers and leaves together is also good. Avoid grass carp, seaweed, mustard greens, peaches, plums, and sparrow meat. (Sengshen Collection Xiangru Baizhu Pills)
⑤ For sudden small holes appearing on the tongue bleeding as if drilled: drink one sheng of Xiangru juice three times daily. (Zhouhoufang)
⑥ For summer heat injury (lying damp and exposed to wind in summer, or excessive raw cold intake, headache, fever, cramps, dry retching, cold limbs, etc.): Xiangru 1 jin, Magnolia Bark (Houpo) (ginger-juice fried), White Hyacinth Bean (Bai Bian Dou) (slightly fried) each 0.5 jin, shred and pulverize. Each dose 5 qian, add 2 bowls of water and 0.5 bowl of wine, decoct to one bowl, let cool in water to settle and take. Take two doses consecutively, very effective. This formula is called Xiangru Drink. The biandou in the formula can be replaced with Coptis (Huanglian) (ginger-juice fried).
⑦ Edema: Xiangru 50 jin, chop into pot, add water and simmer for a long time, remove dregs and concentrate boil until can be rolled into pills the size of a paulownia seed. Each dose 5 pills, taken three times a day; dose may be increased a little each day until urination is smooth. This formula is called Xiangru Decoction. Another formula: Xiangru leaves 1 jin, water 1 dou, boil until soft, remove dregs and reduce to a paste, add 7 liang of Baizhu powder and make into pills the size of a paulownia seed. Each dose 10 pills, take with rice soup. This formula is called Deep Master Xiangru Baizhu Pills.
⑧ For vexation and rib-side pain: pound Xiangru and take 1–2 sheng of juice.
⑨ For unstoppable nasal bleeding: grind Xiangru and take one qian with water.
⑩ For summer-heat vexation and thirst: Xiangru 2 liang. Pound into a powder, each dose 2 qian, water one cup, decoct to seven-tenths, do not remove dregs, take warm at any time. (Shengji Zong Lu Xiangru Powder)
⑾ For water diseases with massive swelling, abdominal fullness, poor appetite: dry Xiangru 50 jin. Finely chop and place in inner pot, cover with water to immerse Xiangru several cun, boil, remove dregs, clarify, reduce over low fire until can be made into pills, take 5 pills the size of paulownia seeds three times daily, gradually increase so that urination is smooth. No worry. (Waitai)
⑿ For infantile localized alopecia areata with no hair growth, sweating and severe pain: concentrate boil old Xiangru juice a little, mix with fat and lead white and apply. (Zimu Secret Records)
⒀ For halitosis: one handful of Xiangru, boil with one dou of water, obtain 3 sheng, slightly swish and gargle. (Qianjin Yaofang)
⒁ For multiple boils and prickly heat: fresh Hua Qizhu appropriate amount, pound and apply externally. (Jiangxi Herbal Manual)
⒂ For skin itching and vulvar eczema: whole plant Hua Qizhu appropriate amount, decoct and wash externally. (Zhejiang Medicinal Plant Records)
⒃ Sword Bean Xiangru Drink:
Sword bean seeds 25 g, Xiangru 10 g, Licorice (Gancao) 3 g. Decoction of the above three herbs in water, add appropriate rock sugar or honey to taste, drink.
Functions
Releases the exterior and warms the middle.
