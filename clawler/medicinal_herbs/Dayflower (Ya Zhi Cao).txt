Cultivation and Preparation of Common Dayflower (Ya zhi cao)
Source
This product is the dried aerial part of the Commelinaceae plant Common Dayflower (Commelina communis L.). Harvest in summer and autumn, sun-dry.
Properties and Flavor
Sweet; Cold; Bland
Processing
Remove impurities, wash clean, cut into sections, and sun-dry.
Appearance
This product can grow up to 60 cm, yellow-green or yellow-white, relatively smooth. Stems have longitudinal ridges, diameter about 0.2 cm, often branched or with adventitious roots, nodes slightly swollen, internodes 3–9 cm long; texture soft, cross-section with pith in the center. Leaves alternate, often wrinkled or fragmented; intact leaves when flattened are ovate-lanceolate or lanceolate, 3–9 cm long and 1–2.5 cm wide; apex acute, margins entire, base extending downward into a membranous leaf sheath that clasps the stem, veins parallel. Flowers are often deciduous; spathes are spathe-like, cordate, sides not joined; petals are crumpled and blue. Slight odor, bland taste.
Effects of Common Dayflower (Ya zhi cao)
Functions
This product is the dried aerial part of the Commelinaceae plant Common Dayflower.
Main indications
Edema, beriberi, dysuria, common cold, erysipelas, mumps, jaundice hepatitis, febrile dysentery, malaria, epistaxis, hematuria, metrorrhagia, leukorrhea, throat swelling and pain, abscesses and sores.

Indications
Promotes urination and clears heat, cools the blood and detoxifies. Treats edema, beriberi, dysuria, common cold, erysipelas, mumps, jaundice hepatitis, febrile dysentery, malaria, epistaxis, hematuria, metrorrhagia, leukorrhea, throat swelling and pain, abscesses and sores.
1. Supplement to the Materia Medica: Used for cold and heat miasma and malaria, phlegm-fluid accumulation, localized swellings, indurated flesh masses, pediatric erysipelas, febrile mania and convulsions, abdominal fullness and distension, body and facial edema, febrile dysentery, snake and dog bites, and toxins of boils and carbuncles.
2. Rihuazi Materia Medica: Boil Common Dayflower with adzuki bean (Chi xiao dou) to reduce dampness in the lower burner and promote urination.
3. Materia Medica of South Yunnan: Tonifies qi and blood, treats women's leukorrhea and excessive uterine bleeding, generates new blood, stops hematuria, epistaxis, and bloody strangury.
4. Essentials of Collected Works: Clears heat-toxin and dissipates abscesses.
5. Compendium of Materia Medica: Relieves throat obstruction.
6. Evolution of Materia Medica: Used in large amounts for acute schistosomiasis with high fever. Also used for acute infectious febrile diseases with fever, impaired consciousness, and cardiac failure.
7. Sichuan Materia Medica: Clears heat and promotes diuresis, moistens the lung. Treats traumatic injuries, muscle and bone pain, and painful urinary dribbling.
8. Guangzhou Military Handbook of Common Chinese Herbs: Treats cardiac edema, beriberi edema, nephritic edema, urinary tract infections and calculi.
Usage and Dosage
Internal use: decoct 15–30 g; fresh product 60–90 g, or crush and take the juice.
External use: appropriate amount, crush and apply as a poultice.
Precautions
Use with caution in those with spleen and stomach deficiency and cold constitution.
Formulas Using Common Dayflower (Ya zhi cao)
1. For urinary obstruction: Bamboo-chicken Herb (Zhu ji cao) 1 liang, Plantain Herb (Che qian cao) 1 liang. Pound to extract juice, add a little honey, take on an empty stomach. (Binhu Collection of Simple Prescriptions)
Bamboo-chicken Herb (Zhu ji cao)
One liang. Pound to extract juice, add a little honey, take on an empty stomach. (Binhu Collection of Simple Prescriptions)
2. For five types of strangury and painful urination: fresh Common Dayflower (Ya zhi cao) young stems and tender leaves 4 liang. Pound, add one cup boiled water, squeeze juice, adjust with honey and take internally three times daily. Reduce dose for physically weak patients. (Quanzhou Materia Medica)
3. For red and white dysentery: Blue-girl Herb (Lan gu cao), decoct and take daily. (Complete Book for Raising Children)
4. For jaundice hepatitis: Common Dayflower (Ya zhi cao) 4 liang, lean pork 2 liang. Stew in water, take the soup and eat the meat, one dose per day. (Jiangxi Herbal)
5. For hypertension: Common Dayflower (Ya zhi cao) 1 liang, broad bean flower (Can dou hua) 3 qian. Decoction, drink as tea. (Jiangxi Herbal)
6. For edema and ascites: fresh Common Dayflower (Ya zhi cao) 2–3 liang. Decoction, take for several consecutive days. (Common Folk Herbs of Zhejiang)
7. For hematemesis: juice of Bamboo-leaf Vegetable (Zhu ye cai) taken internally. (Guiyang Folk Herbal)
8. For intermittent nasal bleeding in atrophic rhinitis and epistaxis: Earth Lotus (Di di ou), decoct and take three times. (Materia Medica of South Yunnan)
9. For throat swelling and pain:
(1) Apply Common Dayflower (Ya zhi cao) juice topically. (Pocket Formulas)
(2) Common Dayflower (Ya zhi cao) 2 liang. Wash, pound to extract juice, frequently hold and swallow. (Jiangxi Herbal)
10. For pediatric erysipelas, febrile dysentery and as an antipyretic in acute febrile disorders: fresh Common Dayflower (Ya zhi cao) 2–3 liang (1 liang if dried); severe cases may use 5–7 liang. Decoction or take the juice. (Common Folk Herbs of Zhejiang)
11. For joint swelling and pain, suppurative swellings and ulcers: fresh Common Dayflower (Ya zhi cao) mashed, add a little strong alcohol (baijiu), apply to the affected area, change once daily. (Common Folk Herbs of Zhejiang)
12. For acute schistosomiasis: fresh Common Dayflower (Ya zhi cao), washed, 5–8 liang daily, decoct and drink as tea, one course is 5–7 days. (Selected Compendium: Infectious Diseases)
13. For snakehead-type sore on the finger: fresh Common Dayflower (Ya zhi cao) and realgar (Xiong huang) mashed, apply to the affected area, change once daily. Early application can disperse swelling; if already suppurated it can reduce swelling and relieve pain. (Quanzhou Materia Medica)
