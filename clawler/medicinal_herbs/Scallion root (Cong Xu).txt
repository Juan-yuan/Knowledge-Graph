Cultivation and Processing of Scallion Fibrous Root (Congxu)
Source
The fibrous roots of the scallion (Cong), a plant of the Liliaceae family.
Taste and Properties
Taste: pungent; nature: neutral.
Effects of Scallion Fibrous Root (Congxu)
Actions
The fibrous roots of the scallion (Cong). Mainly treats wind-cold headache, throat sores, hemorrhoids, and frostbite.
Channels Entered
Lung channel
Indications
Expels wind and disperses cold; detoxifies; disperses stasis. Mainly for wind-cold headache, throat sores, hemorrhoids, and frostbite.
Dosage and Administration
Internal use: decoct as a decoction, 6–9 g; or grind into powder.
External use: appropriate amount, grind to powder and blow on; or decoct in water for fumigation and washing.
