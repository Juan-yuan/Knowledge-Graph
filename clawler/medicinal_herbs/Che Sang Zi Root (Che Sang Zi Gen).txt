Hopbush root (Che sang zai)
Cultivation and processing of Hopbush root (Che sang zi)
Source
It is the root of the hopbush (po liu), a plant of the Sapindaceae (soapberry) family; it can be harvested year-round and used fresh or sun-dried.
Morphology
Che sang zi (the hopbush) is a shrub or small tree, 1–3 m or taller; young branches flattened, with narrow wings or angles, covered with a glutinous mucilage. Leaves simple, alternate; petiole short or nearly sessile; leaf blade papery, shape and size highly variable, linear, linear-spatulate, linear-lanceolate, ob-lanceolate or oblong, 5–12 cm long, 0.5–4 cm wide, apex shortly acuminate, obtuse or rounded, margin entire or with indistinct shallow undulations, both surfaces sticky, glabrous, shiny when dry. Flowers solitary in axils, plant dioecious; inflorescences terminal or axillary on upper parts of branches, shorter than leaves, densely flowered, with angled rachis and branches; pedicels slender, 2–5 mm long, sometimes up to 1 cm; calyx 4, lanceolate or oblong-lanceolate, about 3 mm long, apex obtuse; stamens 7 or 8, filaments under 1 mm, anthers 2.5 mm long, incurved, with glandular points; ovary ovoid, externally covered with a glutinous mucilage, 2- or 3-locular, style about 6 mm long, apex deeply 2- or 3-cleft. Capsule inversely heart-shaped or depressed globose, 2- or 3-winged, wings 1.8–2.5 cm wide, seed coat membranous or papery with veins. Seeds 1 or 2 per locule, lens-shaped, black. Flowering in late autumn, fruiting from late winter to early spring.
Habitat and distribution
Ecological environment: grows on dry hillsides, open ground or sandy soils by the sea.
Resource distribution: distributed in the southeastern, southern to southwestern regions of China.
Taste and properties
Taste bitter; nature cold
Effects of Hopbush root (Che sang zi)
Efficacy
It is the root of the hopbush (po liu) of the Sapindaceae family; mainly used for toothache and wind-toxin streaming conditions.
Indications
Drains fire and detoxifies. Mainly for toothache and wind-toxin streaming down.
Dosage and administration
Internal use: decoction, 15–30 g; fresh material 30–60 g.
Formulas containing Hopbush root (Che sang zi)
1. For toothache: one to two liang of fresh Che sang zai root. Decoction with water for oral administration.
2. For wind-toxin streaming down: one to two liang of dried Che sang zai root, stewed together with an appropriate amount of lean pork and taken as a broth; additionally mash fresh leaves with brown sugar and apply the paste to the affected area.
