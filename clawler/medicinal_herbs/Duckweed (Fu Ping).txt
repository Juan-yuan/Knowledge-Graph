Cultivation and processing of duckweed
Source
This product is the dried whole herb of the Lemnaceae plant Ziping (Spirodela polyrrhiza (L.) Schleid.). Collected from June to September, washed clean, impurities removed, sun-dried.
Taste and property
Pungent, cold.
Characteristics
This product is a flattened leaf-like body, ovate or ovoid, major axis 2–5 mm. Upper surface pale green to gray-green, slightly lateral with one small depression, margin even or slightly curled. Lower surface purple-green to purple-brown, bearing several adventitious roots. Light in weight, easily crushed between fingers. Slight odor, bland taste.
Effects of duckweed
Functions
This product is the dried whole herb of the Lemnaceae plant Ziping.
Main indications: epidemic febrile diseases, rashes that do not surface, wind-heat urticaria, skin itching, edema, urinary retention, sores and ringworm, erysipelas, burns.

Indications
Induce sweating, dispel wind, promote urination, clear heat, detoxify. Treat epidemic febrile diseases, rashes that do not surface, wind-heat urticaria, skin itching, edema, urinary retention, sores and ringworm, erysipelas, burns.
① Benjing (The Classic of Materia Medica): treats violent fever with body itching, expels water qi, superior to wine, promotes beard and hair growth, stops wasting-thirst.
② Bie Lu (Collected Records): brings down qi; used in baths to promote hair growth.
③ Tang Bencao (Tang Materia Medica): treats fire sores.
④ Bencao Shiyi (Supplement to Materia Medica): powdered and applied to the face; crushed juice taken internally treats edema and promotes urination; for poisoning, take dried pingzi powder and take a small spoonful with wine; also used as an ointment to promote hair growth.
⑤ Rihuazi Bencao (Rihuazi Materia Medica): treats heat-toxin wind-heat diseases, heat delirium, melting swellings and toxins, scalds and fire sores, wind rashes.
⑥ Bencao Tujing (Illustrated Materia Medica): treats epidemic febrile diseases, also capable of inducing sweating.
⑦ Diannan Bencao (South Yunnan Materia Medica): induces sweating, detoxifies. Treats scabies, tinea, and dispels wind causing skin itching.
⑧ Bencao Gangmu (Compendium of Materia Medica): treats wind-damp paralysis, beriberi, contusions and injuries, eye redness and membranes, mouth and tongue ulcers, vomiting blood, nosebleed, apoplectic paralysis, erysipelas.
⑨ Yuqiu Yaojie (Yuqiu's Medicinal Explanations): pungent and cooling to release the exterior. Treats epidemic rashes, stroke with facial deviation, paralysis; treats boils and hot swellings, hidden rashes and itching, yangmei-like lesions, acne, tinea versicolor.
⑩ Lingnan Caiyao Lu (Lingnan Drug Collection): for sufferers of wind-itch bathe several times with purple-backed duckweed; for water toxin causing hands and feet up to elbows and knees to be cold, decoct and wash with it.
⑾ Zhongguo Yaoshi Tujian (Illustrated Guide to Chinese Medicinal Plants): crush and apply the juice to insect bites.
Dosage and administration
Internal: decoct, 3–9 g, fresh 15–30 g; or crush and drink the juice; or include in pills or powders.
External: appropriate amount, decoct for fumigating washes; grind to powder and sprinkle or make into a paste to apply.
Precautions and contraindications
Contraindicated for those with exterior deficiency and spontaneous sweating.
1. Ben Cao Jing Shu (Commentary on the Materia Medica): do not use in exterior qi deficiency with spontaneous sweating.
2. Ben Cao Cong Xin (Newly Compiled Materia Medica): do not use lightly unless there is great excess heat.
3. Depei Bencao (Matched Materia Medica): contraindicated in blood deficiency with dry skin, and in qi deficiency with wind-induced pain.
Formulas using duckweed
① For epidemic febrile disease, to induce sweating: Duckweed herb (Fupingcao) 1 liang; Ephedra (Mahuang) (nodes removed, prepared) ½ liang, Cinnamon bark (Rougui) ½ liang, Prepared Aconite (Fuzi) (processed and split, remove navel and skin) ½ liang. Pound the four substances finely and sift. Each dose two qian; use one medium cup of water, fresh ginger half fen, decoct until reduced to six-tenths, take hot with the dregs regardless of timing. (Bencao Tujing)
② For wind-heat on the skin, widespread urticaria: Burdock seed (Niubangzi) and duckweed (Fuping or Fupingcao) equal parts. Use Mint (Bohe) decoction to take two qian, twice daily. (Yangsheng Biyong Fang)
③ For generalized weakness itching: Duckweed powder (Fuping) one qian, yellow duckweed (Huangping) one qian. Decoction with the same Four-Substance Decoction (Siwu Tang) and take. (Danxi Zuanyao)
④ For incessant thirst with vexation and irritability: aquatic duckweed (wash, sun-dry to powder), mix with human breast milk into pills the size of a wutong seed. Take regardless of timing, swallow quickly with congee thirty-nine. (Shenghui Fang)
⑤ For wasting-thirst (diabetes): dried duckweed (Fuping) and Trichosanthes root (Tianhuafen) equal parts. Powder the two ingredients, mix with human breast milk into pills the size of a wutong seed. On an empty stomach take twenty pills, three times a day. (Qianjin Fang — Duckweed Pills)
⑥ For heat toxin: crush duckweed and apply the juice all over. (Zimu Milu)
⑦ For severe wind disease phlegm: 1) Duckweed herb (Fupingcao) harvested in March, washed three to five times, fermented in a pit three to five days, baked into powder, do not expose the mouth; each dose three qian, take with warm wine before meals. 2) In July take purple-backed duckweed (Zibei fuping), sun-dry to powder, half sheng, add to Good Xiaofeng Powder (Xiaofeng San) five liang. Each dose five qian, decoct in water frequently; also use the decoction for bathing. (Shibian Liangfang)
⑧ For abscesses: an appropriate amount of Ziping or purple duckweed (Ziping/Zibei fuping), extract the juice; powdered root bark of Hongjunxiang (Shanmuxie) one tablespoon. Mix and apply externally, thin in the middle and thick on the outside, leaving a hole in the center. (Selected Single and Empirical Prescriptions)
⑨ For urinary obstruction, relieve bladder fullness and edema: surface-collected duckweed, sun-dry, powder, take a small measuring spoonful, three times a day. (Ganjin Fang)
⑩ For acute nephritis: Duckweed herb (Fupingcao) 2 liang, black soybean (Heidou) 1 liang. Decoction in water and drink. (Inner Mongolia: Selected New Medical Methods for Chinese Materia Medica)
⑾ For sores and rashes entering the eye with unbearable pain and fear of damaging the eye: Duckweed herb (Fupingcao), shade-dried to powder, one to two qian per dose; take half a jin of lamb liver, place in a basin, mash and break with a bamboo stick, add half he of water, press out the liver juice, take the medicine after meals with the liver juice. (Children’s Health Total Micro-Discussions — Duckweed Powder)
⑿ For tinea versicolor and abscess wind: purple-backed duckweed (Zibei fuping), sun-dried. Use four liang to decoct for bathing, and rub with duckweed. Alternatively add Han Fangji (Hanfangji) two qian. (Pocket Formulas)
⒀ For powdery spots on the face: small ditch duckweed, make into powder, repeat application several times. (Shenghui Fang)
⒁ To neutralize snake-venom cough entering the abdomen: crush duckweed herb (Fupingcao), squeeze the juice and drink. (Pinhui Jingyao)
⒂ For persistent nosebleed: dry duckweed herb powder, blow into the nostrils. (Shenghui Fang)
⒃ For pterygium encroaching the eye: a small amount of green duckweed (Qingping), grind to a paste, add a little animal brain, apply to the eye. (Shiyi Dexiao Fang)
