Cultivation and Processing of Slender-leaf Ixeris (fen bao ju)
Source
From the whole herb of the Asteraceae plant Slender-leaf Ixeris (xi ye ku mai). Harvested in July to August, washed clean, sun-dried or used fresh.
Original morphology
Slender-leaf Ixeris (xi ye ku mai), a herbaceous plant, 16–50 cm tall. The whole plant is slender. The entire plant is glabrous. Basal leaves in a rosette, linear-lanceolate, 7–15 cm long, 5–9 mm wide, apex gradually acute, base narrowed into a short petiole, margins entire, sometimes ciliate near the base of the margin; upper stem leaves similar to basal leaves but smaller and sessile. Capitula numerous, arranged in corymb-like clusters, with slender peduncles; involucre 5–7 mm long; outer involucral bracts ovate, inner involucral bracts 7–8, linear, apex slightly obtuse; ligulate florets yellow, 7–9 mm long, apex 5-toothed. Achenes fusiform, slightly flattened, brownish, about 3 mm long, with longitudinal ribs, beak short about 1 mm long, pappus light brown, 3–4 mm long.
Habitat and Distribution
Ecology: grows by roadsides, grassy slopes and wastelands.
Resource distribution: distributed in Central China, South China and Pei.
Nature and Taste
Taste: bitter; Nature: slightly cold
Characteristics
Whole herb 10–30 cm long. Stem single or branched at the base. Leaves alternate, folded; when flattened the intact leaves are linear-lanceolate or long linear, 4–15 cm long, 5–9 mm wide, margins entire, nearly sessile. Capitula arranged in corymbiform clusters. Achenes fusiform, brownish, with longitudinal ribs, beak short about 1 mm long. Smell faint, taste bitter.
Effects of Slender-leaf Ixeris (fen bao ju)
Efficacy
From the whole herb of the Asteraceae plant Slender-leaf Ixeris (xi ye ku mai). Main indications: jaundice-type hepatitis, conjunctivitis, and boils.
Indications
Clears heat and detoxifies, relieves pain. Used for jaundice-type hepatitis, conjunctivitis, and boils.
1. "Commonly Used Tibetan Medicinal Herbs": clears heat and detoxifies, anti-inflammatory and analgesic. Treats jaundice-type hepatitis, conjunctivitis, and boils.
2. "Flora of Guangxi": treats cough and skin ulcers/sores.
Usage and Dosage
Internal use: decoction, 6–12 g.
External use: appropriate amount, pounded and applied as a poultice.
