Name
Pericarp of Common Banana (Da Jiao Pi)
Banana Peel (Xiang Jiao Pi)
Cultivation and Preparation of Pericarp of Common Banana (Da Jiao Pi)
Source
It is the pericarp of Musaceae plants such as Common Banana (Da Jiao). The banana peels are collected in autumn and sun-dried.
Morphology
1. Common Banana (Da Jiao), a perennial tufted herb, 3–7 m tall. It has stolons; the pseudostem is thick and heavy, somewhat covered with bloom. Petioles are very long, over 30 cm, often with bloom, with closed leaf wings; leaves erect or held upward, long-elliptic, 1.5–3 m long and 40–60 cm wide, adaxial surface dark green, abaxial surface pale green and conspicuously covered with bloom, base nearly cordate or auriculate and nearly symmetrical, apex acute or acuminate. The spike-like inflorescence is pendulous, rachis glabrous, bracts ovate or ovate-lanceolate, 15–30 cm or longer, deciduous, outer surface purplish-red, inner surface deep red, each bract with two rows of flowers, staminate flowers falling off; perianth pale yellow-white, fused perianth lobes 4–6.5 cm long, free perianth lobes about half the length of the fused lobes, translucent and waxy with luster, oblong or nearly circular, apex with a small mucro, acuminate or rolled into a pouch. The infructescence consists of 7–8 tiers to several dozen tiers of fruit clusters. Fruits oblong, relatively short and stout in length-to-width ratio, body straight or slightly curved, 10–20 cm long, with distinct edges, pedicel usually elongated, flesh fine and firm, astringent before ripening, sweet or slightly sour at maturity but lacking aroma or only slightly aromatic, seedless or with few seeds. Flowering and fruiting occur year-round.
2. Banana (Xiang Jiao), a perennial herb with stolons. Plants are tufted, generally less than 2 m tall, with tall types reaching 4–5 m. Pseudostems are uniformly dark green with black spots and covered with bloom, especially in the upper parts. Petioles short and thick, usually less than 30 cm long, leaf-wing obvious and open, margins brown-red or bright red. Leaf blades long-elliptic, length (1.5–)2–2.2 m, width 60–70 cm, apex obtuse-rounded, base nearly rounded and symmetrical, adaxial surface dark green without bloom, abaxial surface pale green with bloom. Inflorescence pendulous, rachis densely covered with brown pubescence, bract outer surface dark purple, male flower bracts not deciduous, each bract enclosing two rows of flowers; flowers milk-white or slightly pale purple, free perianth lobes nearly circular and entire, apex with an acutely conical tip, the fused perianth bears two small lateral lobes about half the length of the central lobe. The largest bunches may have up to 360 fruits, weighing up to 32 kg; ordinary bunches have 8–10 tiers with 150–200 fruits; fruits oblong, 15–25 cm long, with distinct ridges of 4–5 angles, apex gradually narrowed, peduncle short, peel green, flesh sweet and smooth, seedless, very fragrant. Flowering and fruiting occur year-round.
Habitat and Distribution
1. In China, Fujian, Taiwan, Guangdong, Guangxi and Yunnan all cultivate it. Native to India, Malaysia and other places.
2. Native to southern China. Cultivated in Fujian, Taiwan, Guangdong, Guangxi and Yunnan.
Nature and Flavor
Flavor sweet; astringent; nature cold
Physical Characteristics
Pericarp: fresh is yellow-green, dried is dark brown, appearing as irregular strips and pieces, soft yet tough, with many fibers; bears a relatively long pedicel whose fibers are strong, about 4–5 cm long. The dried, clean specimens are preferred.
Effects of Pericarp of Common Banana (Da Jiao Pi)
Efficacy
It is the pericarp of Musaceae plants such as Common Banana (Da Jiao). Main indications are dysentery, cholera, skin itching, and hypertension.
Indications
Clears heat and detoxifies, lowers blood pressure. Mainly for dysentery, cholera, skin itching, and hypertension.
Dosage and Administration
Internal: decoction, 30–60 g.
External: decoct an appropriate amount for washing; or grind to powder and apply topically.
Discussions by Various Authors
Guangdong Chinese Materia Medica: orally used to treat dysentery. Frying then decocting and drinking treats cholera abdominal pain. Decoction for washing treats skin itching.
