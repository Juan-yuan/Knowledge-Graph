Cultivation and Processing of Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao)
Source
This product is the dried fruit of the Oleaceae plant Forsythia suspensa (Thunb.) Vahl (Lianqiao). Fruits are harvested in autumn when they are beginning to ripen and still green; impurities are removed, they are steamed, then sun-dried, commonly called "green Lianqiao" (Qingqiao); when fruits are fully ripe they are harvested, sun-dried and impurities removed, commonly called "old Lianqiao" (Laoqiao).
Taste and Properties
Bitter, slightly cold.
Description
The product is oblong-ovoid to ovoid, slightly flattened, 1.5–2.5 cm long, 0.5–1.3 cm in diameter. The surface has irregular longitudinal wrinkles and numerous raised small spots, and there is one obvious longitudinal groove on each side. The apex is acuminate; the base has a small fruit stalk or it may have fallen off. Green Lianqiao mostly does not split, surface greenish-brown, fewer raised gray-white small spots, hard texture; seeds numerous, yellow-green, slender, with a wing on one side. Old Lianqiao splits from the apex or into two valves, surface yellow-brown or red-brown, inner surface mostly pale yellow-brown, smooth, with a longitudinal septum; brittle; seeds brown and mostly fallen out. Slight aromatic odor, bitter taste.
Effects of Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao)
Functions
This product is the dried fruit of the Oleaceae plant Forsythia (Lianqiao).
Main indications: warm-heat disorders, erysipelas, rashes, carbuncles and toxic swellings, scrofula, painful urinary dribbling/strangury.
Channels Entered
Enters the Lung meridian, Heart meridian, and Small Intestine meridian.
Indications
Clears heat, resolves toxicity, disperses nodules, reduces swelling. Treats warm-heat disorders, erysipelas, rashes, carbuncles and toxic swellings, scrofula, painful urinary dribbling/strangury.
1. Benjing: "Treats chills and fever, shu-wei, scrofula, carbuncles, malignant sores, goiter, and heat accumulations."
2. Bielu: "Expels white parasites."
3. Yaoxinglun: "Facilitates the five types of urinary dribbling, relieves urinary retention, removes pathogenic heat invading the heart."
4. Rihuazi Materia Medica: "Opens the small intestine, drains pus. Treats sores and boils, relieves pain, and unblocks the menses."
5. Li Gao: "Disperses blood stasis and qi accumulations in the channels; reduces swelling."
6. Wang Haogu: "Treats deafness with a sensation of dullness and fullness."
Usage and Dosage
Internal use: decocted, 6–15 g; or used in pills or powders.
Precautions and Contraindications
Contraindicated for those with spleen and stomach deficiency, fever due to qi deficiency, and for suppurative sores that have ruptured with thin, pale pus.
Formulas Using Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao)
1. For Taiyin wind-warmth, warm-heat, warm epidemic, winter-warm disease in early stage with heat without aversion to cold and with thirst: Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao) 1 liang, Honeysuckle (Jinyinhua) 1 liang, Bitter Platycodon root (Kujiegeng) 6 qian, Mint (Bohe) 6 qian, Lophatherum leaf (Danzhuye) 4 qian, raw Licorice (Gancao) 5 qian, Mustard spike (Jingjie, labeled as Jie sui) 4 qian, Prepared fermented soybean (Dandouchi) 5 qian, Burdock fruit (Niubangzi) 6 qian. Pound into powder; take 6 qian per dose, decoct in fresh reed-root soup; when the aroma emerges take immediately, do not overboil. For severe disease, take approximately every two hours, three times daily, once at night; for mild cases, every three hours, three times daily, once at night; if not resolved, take again. (Wenbing Tiaobian: Yin Qiao San)
2. For all pediatric fevers: Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao), Saposhnikovia root (Fangfeng), Licorice (honey-fried) (Gancao [zhi]), Gardenia fruit (Zhizi) equal parts. Grind into powder, 2 qian per dose, boil in about half a teacup of water to three-quarters, strain and take warm. (Leizheng Huoren Shu: Lianqiao Drink)
3. For red wandering toxin rashes: Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao) alone, decoct and drink. (Yuqiao Yiling)
4. For breast carbuncle and breast lumps: Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao), male-rat excrement (Xiongshushi), Dandelion (Pugongying), Sichuan fritillary bulb (Chuanbeimu) 2 qian each. Decoction and take. (Yuqiao Yiling)
5. For scrofula and indolent lymph nodes: Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao), Guijianyu (Guijianyu), Dianthus (Qumai), Licorice (honey-fried) (Gancao [zhi]) equal parts. Grind to fine powder, 2 qian per dose, take at bedtime with rice-washing water. (Yang family household formula: Lianqiao powder)
6. For tongue fissures and oral ulcers: Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao) 5 qian, Phellodendron bark (Huangbai) 3 qian, Licorice (Gancao) 2 qian. Decoction for gargling. (Yuqiao Yiling)
7. For stagnation of pathogenic heat in the upper and middle jiao causing chest-and-diaphragm heat accumulation, symptoms include body heat, thirst, red face and scorched lips, chest vexation and heat, oral and tongue ulcers, or sore throat with epistaxis, constipation and red urine, or difficult defecation, red tongue with yellow coating, slippery rapid pulse: Scutellaria (Huangqin) and Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao) formula: Scutellaria (Huangqin) 10 liang each, Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao) 2.5 jin. Grind coarsely; 2 qian per dose, one cup of water, add seven bamboo leaves, a little honey, decoct to three-quarters, strain, take warm after meals; children can take half a qian, adjust with age. Stop when bowel movement is achieved. (Local formularies: Liangge San)
8. For mastitis: Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao) 15 g, Dandelion (Pugongying) 30 g, Vaccaria seed (Wangbuluxing) 9 g, Wild chrysanthemum (Yejuhua) 15 g, decoct and take.
9. For intestinal abscess: Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao) 15 g, Scutellaria (Huangqin) 12 g, Gardenia (Zhizi) 12 g, Honeysuckle (Jinyinhua) 18 g, decoct and take. (Formulas 8–9 from Qingdao Handbook of Chinese Materia Medica)
10. For allergic purpura: Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao) 12 g, Red dates 30 g, decoct and take. (Ningxia Handbook of Chinese Materia Medica)
11. For sudden deafness and abrupt loss of hearing: Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao) 1 liang, Xanthium (Cangerzi) 2 liang, decoct to a concentrated decoction and sip slowly. (Yuqiao Yiling)
12. For constipation: Remove stems from dried Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao), wash and dry in the sun, store in a jar. Each time take 15–30 g, steep in water or boil and drink as a tea. Continue for 1–2 weeks, then stop when bowel movement returns. (Shandong Journal of Traditional Chinese Medicine 1985,(5):44)
Notes
1. Forsythia Fruit is cooling and bitter, lightly clearing and ascending; it can treat heat in the upper jiao and is especially effective at detoxifying and resolving carbuncles and dispersing nodules, thus it is an essential herb for sores.
2. When paired with Honeysuckle (Jinyinhua), Mint (Bohe), Schizonepeta (Jingjie) and Licorice (Gancao) it disperses wind and clears heat; when paired with Scrophularia (Xuanshen), Ophiopogon (Maidong), Green lotus heart (Qinglianxin) and Bamboo leaves it clears the heart and drains heat; when paired with Honeysuckle (Jinyinhua), Dandelion (Pugongying), Viola (Zihuadiding) and Red peony root (Chishao) it detoxifies and reduces swelling; when paired with Scrophularia (Xuanshen), Prunella (Xiakucao), and Fritillaria (Beimu) it disperses masses and reduces scrofula.
3. Honeysuckle (Jinyinhua) and Forsythia Fruit (Lianqiao) both have good heat-clearing and detoxifying actions; they can both release exterior heat and clear interior heat and resolve carbuncle toxins, so they are often used together clinically. However, Honeysuckle also cools the blood and stops dysentery, while Forsythia Fruit clears heart heat and disperses nodules and scrofula.
4. In past medicinal practice this product was classified into Forsythia pericarp and Forsythia core (seed). Forsythia pericarp is the fruit body and Forsythia core is the seed; it was generally believed that the core had superior heart-clearing function. In the Shanghai region the classification has been simplified to a single Forsythia product, no longer divided into two medicines.
