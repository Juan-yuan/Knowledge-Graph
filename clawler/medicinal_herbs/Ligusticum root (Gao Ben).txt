Cultivation and Processing of Gaoben
Source
This product is the dried rhizome and root of the Umbelliferae family Ligusticum species Chinese Ligusticum (Gaoben, Ligusticum sinense Oliv.) or Liao Chinese Ligusticum (Liao Gaoben, Ligusticum jeholense Nakai et Kitag.).
Properties (Taste and Nature)
Taste pungent; nature moist
Appearance
(1) Chinese Ligusticum rhizome is irregularly nodular and cylindrical, slightly twisted, slightly branched, 3–10 cm long. Surface brown to dark brown, rough, with longitudinal wrinkles, cork-like bark that peels off easily; the upper end has one to several round hole-like stem bases; the lower side has numerous point-like protruding root scars and remnant roots. Light in weight, relatively hard, breaks easily; fracture surface yellow or yellow-white, fibrous. Strong aromatic odor; taste pungent, bitter, slightly numbing.
(2) Liao Chinese Ligusticum rhizome is irregularly clumped or columnar, 2–10 cm long, 0.5–1.5 cm in diameter. Surface brownish, the upper end has clustered leaf bases and raised nodes, the lower end has many slender curved roots.
Preferably those that are large, relatively thick, firm in texture, and strongly aromatic.
Effects of Gaoben
Functions
This product is the dried rhizome and root of Ligusticum (Gaoben) or Liao Gaoben of the Umbelliferae family.
Main indications: wind-cold headache, vertex pain, cold-damp abdominal pain, diarrhea, hernia and abdominal masses, scabies and tinea.
Channels
Enters
Bladder meridian
Indications
Dispels wind, cold and damp pathogens. Treats wind-cold headache, vertex pain, cold-damp abdominal pain, diarrhea, hernia and abdominal masses, scabies and tinea.
(1) Benjing: Mainly for women’s hernia and abdominal masses, cold in the genital region, swelling and pain, acute abdominal pain; relieves wind headache.
(2) Bielu: Repels mist and dew and moistens; treats dragging wind pathogens and traumatic wounds.
(3) Yaoxinglun: Treats pernicious wind flowing into the lower back causing cold pain; can promote urination, move blood, dispel headaches, snoring and sores.
(4) Rihuazi Bencao: Treats disorders, and skin blemishes, rosacea, and acne.
(5) Yixue Qiyuan: Treats headache, chest pain, and toothache.
(6) Li Gao: Treats wind-damp of the head, face and whole-body skin.
(7) Wang Haogu: Treats diseases of the Governing Vessel, rigidity of the spine and syncope.
(8) Gangmu: Treats abscesses, promotes pus discharge and relieves internal obstruction.
(9) Bencao Zheng: Treats wind-damp diarrhea, wind epilepsy, miasma and pestilence.
(10) Bencao Zaixin: Treats wind-damp itching and pain, headaches and swollen eyes, diarrhea and malarial dysentery.
Dosage and Administration
Internal use: decoct, 3–10 g; or used in pills or powders.
External use: appropriate amount, decoct for wash; or grind to powder and mix for topical application.
Precautions and Contraindications
Contraindicated for headaches due to blood deficiency.
