Cultivation and processing of Asarum (Du Heng)
Source
This herb is the dried whole herb of the Aristolochiaceae plant Asarum forbesii Maxim., Asarum (Du Heng).
Processing
Remove dirt and other impurities from the raw material, cut into short sections, and sieve off ash and debris.
Appearance
This product appears in segments. Roots numerous, slender and cylindrical, diameter 1.5–3 mm; surface gray-yellow to gray-brown, fine longitudinal wrinkles visible, residual rootlets and rootlet scars; cross-section yellow-white.
Rhizomes irregularly nodular or cylindrical-nodular, diameter 2–3 mm, gray-brown, nodes distinct, leafstalk scars and root scars visible.
Leaves few, leaf blades mostly cut, wrinkled or broken, gray-green to brown-green; intact leaves broad cordate, apex blunt or rounded, petioles slender, gray-brown to dark brown.
Brittle in texture. Odor aromatic, especially in the roots and rhizomes; taste pungent and acrid.
Properties and channels entered
Pungent, warm; slightly toxic. Enters the Lung and Kidney channels.
Functions and indications
Dispel wind and scatter cold, relieve pain, transform phlegm and stop cough. Used for wind-cold headache, joint pain, phlegm-retention cough and asthma; externally used for toothache.
Dosage and administration
1–3 g; for external use as appropriate, grind to powder and sprinkle on the affected area.
Efficacy of Asarum (Du Heng)
Efficacy
This herb is the dried whole herb of Asarum forbesii Maxim., Asarum (Du Heng). Used for wind-cold headache, joint pain, phlegm-retention cough and asthma; externally for toothache.
Prescriptions of Asarum (Du Heng)
① For wind-cold headache and initial stages of common cold or influenza characterized by headache and fever: Horse-hoof Fragrance (Ma ti xiang) as powder, each dose one qian (about 3 g), mixed with hot wine and taken; after a short while drink a bowl of hot tea to induce sweating. (From "Xinglin Zhaiyao" — "Essentials of the Apricot Grove"; formula: Xianghan San — Fragrant Sweat Powder)
② For respiratory wheeze and asthma when there is a feeling of stagnation in the chest and disharmony in the epigastrium: Gua Di (melon pedicel) 2 fen, Asarum (Du Heng) 3 fen, Ginseng (Ren Shen) 1 fen; pound and sieve, take one qian (about 3 g) with decoction, two or three times daily. (From "Buque Zhouhou Fang" — "Supplement to the Post-Elbow Formulas")
③ For wheeze and panting: Horse-hoof Fragrance (Ma ti xiang), dry-roast and grind into fine powder, each dose 2–3 qian; if during an acute attack, mix with light vinegar and take, expect effect when phlegm and saliva are expelled. (From "Puji Fang" — "Universal Relief Formulas"; formula: Hei Ma Ti Xiang San — Black Horse-hoof Fragrance Powder)
④ For summer rashes: Asarum root (powdered) 3–4 fen. Swallow with boiling water.
⑤ For traumatic pain and snakebite: Asarum (powdered) take 2 fen each time; externally use fresh Asarum, pounded and applied to the affected area.
⑥ For snakebite: Asarum root 1–2 qian, Qingpeng leaves (Asteraceae: Artemisia species) and Zhuyexiqing leaves (Orchidaceae: striped-leaf orchid) in equal amounts, Honeysuckle (Jin Yin Hua) 3–4 qian, Yegangzi (Ye gang zi; Loganiaceae/"drunken-fish herb") 5–6 qian; decoct in water and take three times daily before meals.
⑦ For sores and toxins: Asarum root and Qingpeng leaves 1–2 qian each; pound and apply to the affected area. (Formulas 4 and following from "Zhejiang Tianmu Mountain Materia Medica")
⑧ For unnamed swellings and carbuncles, early-stage vine-carbuncle presenting as diffuse swelling without a head, hard pain without redness, and spreading lesions: seven fresh Asarum leaves, rinse briefly with boiling water, simmer for one hour, take once daily to produce slight sweating; mash the residue, heat and apply as a poultice. (From "Fujian Folk Materia Medica")
⑨ For dental caries pain: fresh Asarum leaves chewed or mashed and packed into the carious cavity. (From "Fujian Folk Materia Medica")
