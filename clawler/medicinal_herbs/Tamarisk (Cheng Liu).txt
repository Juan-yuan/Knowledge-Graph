Cultivation and preparation of Chinese tamarisk (cheng liu)
Source
This item is the tender twigs and leaves of the Tamaricaceae plant Chinese tamarisk (cheng liu). Harvest tender twigs and leaves in May to June and shade-dry.
Properties and flavor
Taste: sweet; pungent; Nature: neutral
Processing
Remove impurities, remove stems, spray-moisten then cut into sections, sun-dry.
Description
Branches slender and cylindrical, diameter 0.5–1.5 mm, surface yellow-green, nodes relatively dense, leaves fresh and membranous, awl-shaped or ovate-lanceolate, 1–3 mm long, with a keel-like ridge on the underside. Texture brittle, easily broken, fracture surface yellow-white, center contains pith. Aroma faint, taste mild. Best quality is slender tender branches and green-colored leaves.
Effects of Chinese tamarisk (cheng liu)
Efficacy
This item is the tender twigs and leaves of the Tamaricaceae plant Chinese tamarisk (cheng liu).
Main indications: wind-heat common cold; early-stage measles; rashes not fully erupted; wind-damp painful obstruction; skin itching

Indications
Disperses wind; releases the exterior; promotes eruption of rashes; detoxifies. Indicated for wind-heat common cold; early-stage measles; rashes not fully erupted; wind-damp painful obstruction; skin itching
Usage and Dosage
Internal use: decoction, 10–15 g; or included in powdered formulas.
External use: appropriate amount, decoct and use to wash or rub.
Precautions and contraindications
Contraindicated when measles has fully erupted or in patients who are weak and sweating profusely.
