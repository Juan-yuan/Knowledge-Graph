Cultivation and Processing of Honeysuckle Flower (Jin Yin Hua)
Source
The flower buds of plants of the Caprifoliaceae family, Lonicera species. Pick the buds in May to June on clear mornings just after the dew has dried; spread on mats to sun-dry or dry in the shade, turning frequently to prevent blackening. Avoid exposure to strong sunlight. Store in a dry, well-ventilated place to prevent insect infestation and discoloration.
[Habitat and Distribution]
Produced in most regions of China, with Shandong having the largest output and Henan producing the better quality.
Properties and Taste
Sweet; cold
Characteristics
Dried flower buds are long, rod-like, slightly curved, about 2–3 centimeters long, thicker at the upper end, about 1.5–3 millimeters in diameter. The exterior is yellow or yellow-brown, covered with short soft hairs and glandular hairs. At the base are small green calyces, 5-lobed; the lobes are triangular and hairless. When the bud is split open, five stamens and one pistil are visible. The corolla is bilabiate; the stamens and pistil extend like beards. Aromatic odor, slightly bitter taste. Best when the flowers are unopened, yellow-white, and plump.
Effects of Honeysuckle Flower (Jin Yin Hua)
Efficacy
The flower buds of Lonicera species of the Caprifoliaceae family.
Main indications: febrile disease fever, dysentery due to heat-toxin, carbuncles and boils, swellings and toxins, scrofula, hemorrhoids and anal fistula.
Meridians
Lung Meridian; Stomach Meridian
Indications
Clears heat and relieves toxicity. Treats fever in febrile diseases, dysentery due to heat-toxin, carbuncles and boils, swellings and toxins, scrofula, hemorrhoids and anal fistula.
① Dian Nan Ben Cao: clears heat, treats various sores, carbuncles and back eruptions, vaginal discharge, and scrofula.
② Sheng Cao Yao Xing Bei Yao: can disperse carbuncles and sores, stop dysenteric diarrhea, wash criptic sores, and remove heat in the skin and blood.
③ Ben Cao Bei Yao: nourishes blood and stops thirst. Treats scabies and tinea.
④ Chongqing Tang Miscellaneous Notes: clears collaterals from wind-fire-damp-heat, resolves febrile epidemics and foul turbidity, calms liver and gallbladder rising wind yang, treats convulsions and epilepsy.
⑤ Guangzhou Military Common Chinese Herbs Manual: clears heat and detoxifies. Treats external contraction fever and cough, enteritis, bacillary dysentery, measles, mumps, septicemia, sores and boils, appendicitis, wound infections, and infantile prickly toxin. Prepared as a cold herbal tea, it can prevent heatstroke, colds, and intestinal infectious diseases.
Dosage and Administration
Internal use: decoct as a tea, 10–20 g; or used in pills or powders.
External use: appropriate amount, crushed and applied as a poultice.
Precautions and Contraindications
Contraindicated for spleen and stomach deficiency cold and for ulcers or sores with clear pus due to qi deficiency.

[Dietary Therapy Recipes]
1. Honeysuckle and Mung Bean Drink
Mung beans 60 g, boil to extract juice; add Honeysuckle Flower (Jin Yin Hua) 10 g and appropriate honey. Simmer 20–30 minutes, strain and drink.
Function: clears heat, relieves vexation, relieves summer heat and thirst. Indications: summer-heat vexation and thirst, short and reddish urination, or prickly heat and itching. Also used to help prevent heatstroke.
2. Chrysanthemum, Honeysuckle and Dandelion Tea
Chrysanthemum Flower (Ju Hua) 15 g, Honeysuckle Flower (Jin Yin Hua) 20 g, Dandelion (Pu Gong Ying) 30 g, Licorice Root (Gan Cao) 3 g, Mung Beans 20 g. Put all ingredients into a pot, decoct and strain to obtain the liquid. One dose daily, use continuously for 5–7 days as one course.
Function: clears heat and detoxifies, anti-infective. Indication: early-stage mastitis in women with significant heat-toxin.
3. Two-Flower Tea
Honeysuckle Flower (Jin Yin Hua) 50 g, Hang Chrysanthemum (Hang Ju Hua) 100 g, honey to taste. Pick out impurities from the honeysuckle and chrysanthemum, rinse and lightly fry in a pot, cool and store in a clean jar. When needed, take a small amount, place in a cup, pour boiling water and steep, sweeten with honey as desired. Take 2–3 times daily.
Function: clears heat and dispels wind, brightens the eyes and lowers blood pressure. Indications: summer heat vexation and thirst, hypertension, coronary heart disease, palpitations and insomnia, sore throat, cold and cough. Also suitable as a cooling summer beverage.
4. Ren Dong Decoction (Honeysuckle Decoction)
Honeysuckle Flower (Jin Yin Hua) 120 g, Licorice Root (Gan Cao) 45 g. Decoct in water or wine and take as a tea. Indication: treats "all internal and external swellings and abscesses," such as intestinal abscesses, mastitis, and boils.
