Cultivation and Processing of Coriander Fruit (Yuan Sui Zi)
Source
This product is the dried mature fruit of the Apiaceae (Umbelliferae) plant coriander (Yuan Sui; Coriandrum sativum L.).
Characteristics
This product is a schizocarp, globose, 3–5 mm in diameter. Surface pale yellow-brown to yellow-brown, with ten relatively distinct longitudinal secondary ribs and ten less distinct, wavy and curved primary ribs arranged alternately; the apex shows very short remnants of the style and traces of five sepal teeth; the base often retains a small pedicel or pedicel scar about 15 mm long. The ventral surface of each mericarp is centrally concave, with three longitudinal ridges, the central one relatively straight and the two lateral ones curved in an arc. Texture hard; when crushed by hand it emits a characteristic strong aroma and tastes slightly pungent.
Nature, Flavor, and Meridian Entry
Pungent, neutral. Enters the Lung and Stomach channels.
Functions and Indications
Releases the exterior, promotes eruption of rashes, and stimulates the appetite. Used for common cold with nasal congestion, poor eruption of measles/pox, poor appetite, and toothache.
Effects of Coriander Fruit (Yuan Sui Zi)
Actions
This product is the dried mature fruit of the Apiaceae plant coriander. Used for common cold with nasal congestion, poor eruption of measles/pox, poor appetite, and toothache.
Dosage and Administration
Internal use: decocted, 6–12 g; or added to powdered formulas.
External use: decoction for gargling or for fumigation/washing.
Prescriptions of Coriander Fruit (Yuan Sui Zi)
1. For early-stage measles eruption: Coriander Fruit (Yuan Sui Zi) four liang. After pounding, put into a fired earthenware pot or an aluminum pot, fill with clear water, place in the patient’s room (the room should be small and not ventilated), boil with charcoal so that steam fills the room, adding charcoal and water as needed; stop when the measles have fully erupted. (Zhejiang Journal of Traditional Chinese Medicine (3):32, 1959)
2. Treats dysentery and bloody diarrhea: Coriander Fruit (Yuan Sui Zi) one he. Pound and crush; for the red type mix with sugar water, for the white type mix with fresh ginger juice (Fresh Ginger, Sheng Jiang) and take warm. One prescription instructs taking it mixed with wine. (Pu Ji Fang)
3. For intestinal wind with continuous rectal bleeding that becomes hemorrhoids: Coriander Seed (Hu Sui Zi), Psoralea Fruit (Bu Gu Zhi) each half liang. Pound and sift the two ingredients into a powder. Each dose two qian, taken with rice water made from aged rice, before meals. (Sheng Ji Zong Lu: Hu Sui Zi Powder)
4. For hemorrhoids: Coriander Seed (Hu Sui Zi) stir-fried, finely ground, mixed with wine; take three to five doses. (Hai Shang Fang)
5. For rectal prolapse and anal fistula: Coriander Seed (Hu Sui Zi) one sheng, Frankincense (Ru Xiang) a small amount, millet bran (su kang) half sheng or one sheng. Use the above to form a clay furnace leaving only a small hole about the size of the anus, preventing smoke from escaping, and fumigate. (Ru Men Ting Qin)
6. For prolapsed rectum: In autumn and winter pound Coriander Seed (Hu Sui Zi), cook with vinegar and apply as a hot compress. (Shi Liao Ben Cao)
7. For toothache: Coriander Seed (Hu Sui Zi), with five sheng of water, boil down to one sheng, use to gargle and then spit out. (Bei Ji Fang)
