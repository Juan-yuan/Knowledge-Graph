Sugarcane chicken (Zhe Ji)
Source: https://zhongyibaike.com/wiki/%E8%94%97%E9%B8%A1
Name
Sweetcane Bud (Zheji)
Cultivation and Processing of Sweetcane Bud (Zheji)
Source
Tender buds that grow from the nodes of sugarcane (ganzhe) of the Poaceae family. Harvested in summer.
Morphology
Sugarcane (ganzhe) is a perennial herbaceous grass. Stems about 3 m high, 2–5 cm thick, green or brownish-red, stems below the inflorescence bearing white silky hairs. Leaf sheaths longer than the internodes, glabrous except for hairs at the sheath mouth; ligule membranous, truncate, about 2 mm long; leaf blades flat, glabrous on both surfaces, with a white thickened midrib, 40–80 cm long and about 20 mm wide. Inflorescence large, up to 60 cm long, main axis with white silky hairs; rachilla internodes 7–12 mm long, margins sparsely ciliate; sessile spikelets lanceolate, 4.5–5 mm long, the receptacle bearing silky hairs 2–3 times longer than the spikelets; the upper part of the glumes membranous, margins with small hairs; the first glume apex somewhat obtuse, with 2 ridges and 4 veins; the second glume boat-shaped, with 3 veins and an acute apex; the first palea oblong-lanceolate, with 1 vein and an acute apex; the second palea narrow and linear, about 3 mm long; the second lemma lanceolate, about 2 mm long. Pedicellate spikelets similar to sessile spikelets; pedicels 3–4 mm long, glabrous, slightly dilated at the apex. Flowering and fruiting in autumn.
Habitat and Distribution
A commonly cultivated plant throughout southern China.
Effects of Sweetcane Bud (Zheji)
Efficacy
Tender buds that grow on the nodes of sugarcane (ganzhe) of the Poaceae family. Mainly used to treat xiaoke (thirsting disorder/diabetes).
Indications
Clears heat and generates fluids. Primarily indicated for xiaoke (thirsting disorder/diabetes).
Dosage and Administration
Internal use: decoction, 60–90 g.
Prescriptions of Sweetcane Bud (Zheji)
For treating diabetes: Sweetcane Bud (Zheji) 3 liang. Five bowls of water, decocted down to one bowl, take warm at intervals.
