Cultivation and Processing of Tongguang Powder
Source
The stems, roots, or leaves of Tongguan vine (Tongguanteng), a plant of the Asclepiadaceae family. Stems are harvested in autumn and winter. Remove the corky bark, slice and sun-dry. Roots may be harvested year-round.
Morphology
Deciduous climbing vine, 2–6 m long. Roots stout, woody, cylindrical, long with few branches, bark gray-brown. Stems stout and long; lower parts cylindrical, pale yellowish-brown; upper parts green, compressed-cylindrical, with two distinct opposite longitudinal grooves; all parts exude white latex when broken; young branches densely covered with pale yellow soft hairs. Leaves opposite; cordate, 8–14 cm long, 5–10 cm wide, apex acute or acuminate, margin entire or undulate, underside with short pale yellow or gray-white soft hairs; petiole 4–6 cm long. Many-flowered axillary umbels; flowers small, petals reddish-yellow. Follicles spindle-shaped, paired, 5–9 cm long, densely covered with gray-yellow tomentum. Seeds with silky hairs. Flowering in summer.
Habitat and Distribution
Grows in mixed broadleaf forest on sunny slopes or climbs on rock faces. Distributed in Yunnan.
Properties and Flavor
Selected Chinese Herbal Medicines of Yunnan: bitter, slightly cold.
Efficacy of Tongguang Powder
Actions
The stems, roots, or leaves of Tongguan vine (Tongguanteng), Asclepiadaceae. Indicated for pneumonia, bronchitis, bronchial asthma, pharyngitis, tonsillitis, cystitis, and for treating sores, swellings and toxic ulcers.
Channels entered
Lung; Liver meridians
Indications
Clears heat and detoxifies; stops cough and calms asthma. Treats pneumonia, bronchitis, bronchial asthma, pharyngitis, tonsillitis, cystitis, and treats sores, swellings and toxic ulcers.
1) Selected Chinese Herbal Medicines of Yunnan: anti-inflammatory, stops cough, calms asthma, anticancer. Treats bronchitis, bronchial asthma, various cancers, rheumatism, traumatic injuries, fractures, retinal disorders, and stomach disease.
2) Selected Chinese Herbal Medicines of Simao, Yunnan: clears heat and detoxifies, anti-inflammatory and analgesic. Treats pharyngitis, gastrointestinal heat accumulation, pneumonia, tonsillitis, cystitis.
3) Guizhou Medicinal Plant Catalogue: invigorates blood and promotes tissue regeneration, promotes lactation, stops cough. Treats cough, traumatic injuries, tendon and bone fractures, and insufficient lactation.
Dosage and Administration
Internal use: decoction 9–15 g; or grind into powder.
External use: appropriate amount of fresh leaves, pounded and applied.
Prescriptions Using Tongguang Powder
1) For laryngitis and oral ulceration: soak Big bitter vine (Dakuteng) in water and take orally. (Selected Chinese Herbal Medicines of Simao, Yunnan)
2) For gastroenteritis, stomach pain, and jaundice-type hepatitis: rhizome of Flat vine (Bianteng) one liang. Decoction and take. (Honghe Chinese Herbal Medicines)
3) For carbuncles, sores and toxic swellings: appropriate amount of fresh leaves of Tongguang Powder, pounded and applied externally. (Economic Plants of Yunnan)
4) For various cancers: Tongguang Powder 1 liang to 1 liang 5 qian,
White pepper (Bai Hu Jiao)
ten pieces. Decoction for oral administration. (Selected Chinese Herbal Medicines of Yunnan)
