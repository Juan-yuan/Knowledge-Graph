Cultivation and Processing of Kuding Tea (Kǔdīngchá)
Source
Mainly the leaves of holly family (Aquifoliaceae) plants: Gougu (Gǒugǔ) and Large-leaved Holly (Dàyè Dōngqīng).
Properties and Taste
Sweet; Bitter; Cold
Processing
Remove impurities, shade-dry, set aside.
Characteristics
① Gougu leaves; also called: Horn-spine Tea (Jiǎocìchá) (Gangmu Shiyi). See the Gougu leaf entry for detailed appearance. Leaves from old Gougu trees are also used; leaf blades are ovate-elliptic, apex shortly acute, base rounded, upper surface smooth, coriaceous and thick. Produced in Jiangsu, Zhejiang and other regions.
② Large-leaved Holly leaves; also called: Kudeng Tea (Kǔdēngchá) (Ducao Qiuniu). Leaves are ovate-oblong elliptic, coriaceous, not wrinkled, some longitudinally slightly curled, upper surface yellow-green or gray-green and glossy, underside yellow-green, taste slightly bitter. Produced in Zhejiang, Fujian, Guangxi and other regions.
Effects of Kuding Tea
Therapeutic Effects
Mainly the leaves of holly family (Aquifoliaceae) plants: Gougu (Gǒugǔ) and Large-leaved Holly (Dàyè Dōngqīng).
Main indications: headache, toothache, red eyes, blocked ears, febrile disease with vexation and thirst, dysentery
Meridians

;

;
Stomach meridian
Indications
Disperse wind-heat, clear the head and eyes, relieve vexation and thirst. Treats headache, toothache, red eyes, blocked ears, febrile disease with vexation and thirst, dysentery.
① Benjing Fengyuan: stops dysentery.
② Yilin Zuanyao: treats febrile mania.
③ Gangmu Shiyi: expel wind, invigorate blood, induce abortion.
④ Bencao Zaixin: reduce food stagnation and transform phlegm, relieve vexation and stop thirst, promote urination and bowel movements, remove greasiness.
⑤ Bencao Qiuyuan: clear Lung and Spleen, stop dysentery, clear head and eyes.
⑥ Zhongguo Yixue Da Cidian: disperse liver wind, clear head and eyes, treat tinnitus, deafness, blocked ears with purulent discharge, activate blood vessels, cool the uterus.
⑦ Sichuan Materia Medica: can clear heat and disperse wind, relieve vexation and quench thirst. Treats headache, toothache, tinnitus, red eyes, and food stagnation with phlegm.
Usage and Dosage
Internal: decoct, 3–9 g; or include in pill formulations.
External: appropriate amount, decocted water for fumigation and washing, or apply topically.
Precautions and Contraindications
Use with caution in those with spleen and stomach deficiency-cold.
Prescriptions using Kuding Tea
① For treating pulmonary tuberculosis: Gougu tender leaves one liang. Roast dry, steep in boiling water, drink as tea. (Hunan Materia Medica)
② For treating lumbar and joint pain: Gougu leaves soaked in wine and drunk. (Hunan Materia Medica)
