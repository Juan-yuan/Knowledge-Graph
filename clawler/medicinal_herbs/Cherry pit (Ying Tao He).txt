Cultivation and processing of Cherry kernel (Yingtao he)
Source
This item is the dried mature fruit stone of Cherry (Yingtao) of the Rosaceae family. In summer, mature fruits are placed in a jar and rubbed with implements to separate the flesh from the stones; the flesh is then washed away, the clean stones are taken and sun-dried.
[Morphology]
Cherry (Yingtao) is a deciduous shrub or tree, 3–8 m tall. Bark gray-white with obvious lenticels; young shoots glabrous or with sparse soft hairs. Leaves alternate; petiole 0.7–1.5 cm long, with sparse soft hairs, apex with one or two large glands; stipules lanceolate, with pinnatifid glandular teeth, early deciduous; leaf blade ovate or oblong-ovate, 5–12 cm long, 3–5 cm wide, apex gradually acuminate or caudate-acuminate, base rounded, margin with sharp coarse serrations, tooth apices with small glands, adaxial surface dark green and nearly glabrous, abaxial surface pale green with sparse soft hairs along veins or between veins; flowers bisexual, inflorescences umbel-like or nearly umbellate; 3–6 flowers, opening before the leaves; pedicel 8–19 mm long, with sparse soft hairs; hypanthium campanulate, externally with sparse soft hairs; calyx lobes 5, triangular-ovate or ovate-lanceolate; apex acute or obtuse; petals 5, white, obovate, apex concave or bifid; stamens 30–35, up to 50 in cultivation; style nearly as long as stamens, glabrous; pistil 1, ovary inferior. Drupe nearly globose, red, 9–13 mm in diameter, one seed enclosed in a yellowish-white woody endocarp. Flowering period March–April. Fruiting period May–June.
Characteristics
This item is flat-ovoid or oblong-ovoid, length 0.6–0.9 cm, 0.7–1 cm, diameter 0.5–0.7 cm. Surface pale yellow to gray-yellow, with slightly raised reticulate texture; one end pointed, slightly beak-like, the other end with an oblong pit; dorsal suture line slightly prominent, ventral suture line distinctly prominent, each side with a longitudinal raised rib, edge with a ridge line. Texture hard. When broken, one seed is visible, flat-ovoid, yellow-brown, surface with fine wrinkles. Seed coat thin, two cotyledons, pale yellow, rich in oil. Smell slight, taste slightly bitter.
[Nature, Taste, and Meridian Affinities]
Pungent, neutral in nature. Enters the Lung channel.
[Functions and Indications]
Disperses and promotes the eruption of rashes; detoxifies. Indicated for early-stage measles with incomplete eruption.
[Usage and Dosage]
Internal use: decocted as a decoction, 4.5–9 g, crushed at time of use.
External use: apply the pressed juice topically or use the decoction to wash.
Effects of Cherry kernel (Yingtao he)
Efficacy
This item is the dried mature fruit stone of Cherry (Yingtao) of the Rosaceae family. Indicated for early-stage measles with incomplete eruption.
Precautions and Contraindications
Illustrated Materia Medica of Southern Yunnan: avoid in yang-type pox conditions.
Prescriptions using Cherry kernel (Yingtao he)
1. For treating hoarseness after pox eruption: Sweet cherry kernels (Tian yingtao he), twenty pieces. Roast in an earthenware pot until yellow, decoct and drink. (Supplement to the Compendium of Materia Medica)
2. For treating eyelid tumors: grind Cherry kernels (Yingtao he) with water and apply the paste; the tumor gradually disappears. (Medical Guide)
