Cultivation and Processing of Fermented Soybean (Dan Dou Chi)
Source
A fermented processed product of the mature seeds of the soybean, a plant of the Leguminosae (legume) family.
Properties and Flavors
Bitter; Acrid; Neutral
Appearance
This product is ellipsoid and slightly flattened, length 0.6-1 cm, diameter 0.5-0.7 cm.
Surface black, wrinkled and uneven, dull, with a brown strip-like hilum on one side, micropyle not obvious.
Two cotyledons, thick.
Texture soft, fracture surface brown-black.
Slight odor, slightly sweet taste.
Those with large, plump grains and black color are preferred.
Preparation
Decoct mulberry leaves (Sang Ye) and Artemisia annua (Qing Hao) in water, filter; mix the decoction with washed black soybeans, and after the liquid is absorbed, place in a steamer and steam thoroughly; remove and cool slightly, then place in a container, cover with the residuum of the decocted mulberry leaves and Artemisia annua, seal and allow to ferment until a yellow coat forms; remove and sun dry to obtain the product. (For each 100 jin of black soybeans, use 4 jin mulberry leaves and 7 jin Artemisia annua.)
Besides the above processing method for Light Douchi, other herbs such as Polygonum hydropiper (La Liao), Eupatorium fortunei (Pei Lan), Perilla leaf (Su Ye), Agastache (Huo Xiang), Ephedra (Ma Huang), Artemisia annua (Qing Hao), Notopterygium (Qiang Huo), Bupleurum (Chai Hu), Angelica dahurica (Bai Zhi), Ligusticum (Chuan Xiong), Kudzu root (Ge Gen), Radix Paeoniae Rubra (Chi Shao), Platycodon root (Jie Geng), Licorice (Gan Cao), etc., may be used; either decocted to obtain the herbal juice to cook the beans, or the herbs ground into powder and mixed with cooked soybeans and then sealed to ferment—various processing methods exist.
Effects of Fermented Soybean (Dan Dou Chi)
Efficacy
A fermented processed product of the mature seeds of soybean.
Mainly treats typhoid and febrile diseases, chills and fever, headache, restlessness, chest oppression.

Indications
Releases the exterior, eliminates vexation, disperses constraint, detoxifies. Treats typhoid and febrile diseases, chills and fever, headache, irritability, chest oppression.
① Bielu: Treats typhoid headache with chills and fever, miasmatic and noxious influences, restlessness and fullness and oppression, consumptive panting and gasping, and cold painfulness of both feet.
② Yaoxing Lun: Treats epidemic febrile disease with sweating; when roasted and powdered, can stop night sweats and remove vexation; raw pounded into pills and taken, treats alternating cold and heat and wind, and sores in the chest; boiled and taken, treats bloody dysentery and abdominal pain.
③ Rihuazi Ben Cao: Treats medicinal poisoning, malaria, steaming bone disorder; also treats dog bites.
④ Zhenzhu Nang: Removes vexation in the heart, typhoid headache, irritability.
⑤ Bencao Gangmu (Compendium of Materia Medica): Descends qi, harmonizes the middle. Treats typhoid toxic febrile eruptions and vomiting with counterflow.
⑥ Benjing Fengyuan: Compress and squeeze juice with water to treat poisoning from accidentally ingesting bird or beast livers.
⑦ Huiyue Yijing: Stabilizes pregnancy.
Dosage and Administration
Internal use: decoct as a decoction, 5–15 g; or incorporate into pill formulas.
External use: appropriate amount, pounded and applied topically; or stir-fry until charred, grind into powder and apply as a paste.
Precautions and Contraindications
① Yaoxing Lun: If it acquires a sour smell, it is considered good.
② Ben Cao Jing Shu: In cases where a typhoid disease has transmitted into the yin meridians and involved the central three yin, it is not appropriate to use.
Prescriptions of Fermented Soybean (Dan Dou Chi)
① For restless vexation and inability to sleep after sweating and purging, with vexation in the heart:
Gardenia (Zhi Zi)
Fourteen pieces (split), fragrant douchi four heaped measures (wrapped in cotton). For the above two ingredients, use 4 sheng of water, first decoct the Gardenia to obtain 2.5 sheng, add the douchi, boil down until reduced to 1 sheng, remove dregs. Divide into two doses; take one warm, if it induces vomiting stop, then take the second after vomiting ceases. (Shang Han Lun: Zhi Zi Chi Tang)
② For febrile toxic eruptions and severe epidemics difficult to treat: Black paste
Raw Rehmannia (Sheng Di Huang)
Half jin (chopped), good douchi one sheng, pork fat two jin. Boil together five or six boils, reduce the liquid by one third, strain out dregs, powder; add Realgar (Xiong Huang) and Musk (She Xiang) each in amounts the size of a soybean, mix well and take it all; the poison will come out through the skin. (Bu Que Zhou Hou Fang)
③ For sudden severe diarrhea from typhoid and stagnant dysentery with abdominal pain: Douchi one sheng,
Allium macrostemon bulb (Xie Bai) one handful (cut into inch pieces). Use the above two items with 3 sheng of water, boil until the Allium is cooked, remove dregs, divide and take twice; if not cured repeat. (Fan Wang Fang: Chi Xie Tang)
④ For many years of wheeze and shortness of breath of the lung, gasping cough, inability to sleep morning and evening: Arsenic (Pi Shi) 1.5 qian (pulverized to a fine powder), Douchi (good quality) 1.5 liang (slightly moisten with water for a short time, press between paper to dry, grind into a paste). Pound the paste and the arsenic together until extremely uniform, make pills the size of mustard seeds. Each dose fifteen pills; children's doses according to size, and take with very cold la tea while lying down and swallowing, use as directed. (Ben Shi Fang: Zi Jin Dan)
⑤ For breast engorgement after weaning: Douchi half jin, decoct in water, take one small bowl, use the remainder to wash the breasts. (Guangxi Collection of New Medical Methods for Chinese Herbal Prescriptions)
⑥ For pediatric erysipelas that breaks into ulcers with yellow discharge: Char-fry douchi until smoking stops, grind into powder, mix with oil and apply as a topical dressing. (Yao Hezhong)
⑦ For painful ulceration on the penis: Douchi one fen, earthworm in wet mud two fen, pound with water into a paste and apply, allow to dry and repeat; avoid hot foods such as Chinese chives and garlic. (Yaoxing Lun)
