Cultivation and Processing of Scallion Leaf (Cong Ye)
Source
Leaves of the scallion plant (family Liliaceae).
Properties and Flavors
Qianjin • Food Treatment: warm, pungent.
Effects of Scallion Leaf (Cong Ye)
Efficacy
Leaves of the scallion plant (family Liliaceae). Indications: wind-cold common cold, headache and nasal congestion, fever without sweating; stroke, facial edema; sores and carbuncles with swelling and pain; traumatic injuries from falls and blows.
Main Indications
Dispel wind and induce sweating, detoxify and reduce swelling. Treat wind-cold common cold, headache and nasal congestion, fever without sweating; stroke, facial and ocular edema; sores and carbuncles with swelling and pain; traumatic injuries from falls and blows.
1) Qianjin • Food Treatment: green leaves benefit the eyes. Expel pathogenic qi from the liver, calm the middle and tonify the five viscera, benefit the eye's essence (the character 'jing' is cited as 'jing' meaning 'eye' in Zhenghe Materia Medica), kill the poisons of a hundred medicines.
2) Dietary Materia Medica: mainly for febrile disease with intense heat, sweating from windstroke, facial and ocular edema, pain in bones, joints and head.
3) Rihuazi Materia Medica: stems and leaves pounded with salt and applied as a compress for snake and insect bites and cuts. For chapping and swelling, add water, roast and grind, then apply as a poultice.
4) Materia Medica Illustrated: roasted scallion treats bruises and traumas.
Dosage and Administration
Internal use: decoct, 9–15 g.
External use: pounded and applied as a poultice, hot compress, or decoction for washing.
Formulas containing Scallion Leaf (Cong Ye)
1) For sores with wind-water swelling and pain: Scallion leaf (Cong Ye),
Dried ginger (Gan Jiang)
Phellodendron bark (Huang Bai)
Combine and decoct together into a soup; use to soak and wash. (Dietary Materia Medica)
2) For edema of both feet due to water disorder: shred scallion leaves and stems, boil until softened and pulpy, apply three to five times daily. (Du Xing Fang)
3) For substitute finger: withered yellow scallion leaves, boil and soak them. (Qianjin Prescriptions)
