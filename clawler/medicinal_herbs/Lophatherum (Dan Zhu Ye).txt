Cultivation and processing of Common Lophatherum Herb (Dan Zhu Ye)
Source
This product is the dried stems and leaves of the grass family plant Common Lophatherum Herb (Dan Zhu Ye).
Properties and flavor
Sweet; bland; cold
Characteristics
Dried leafy stems and branches, total length 30–60 centimeters. Commercial material is often cut. Stems are grayish-yellow, hollow, flattened-cylindrical in shape, diameter 1–2 millimeters; jointed, leaf sheaths clasp the stem, with long white soft hairs along the edges. Leaves are lanceolate, wrinkled and curled, length 5–20 centimeters, width 2–3.5 centimeters, bluish-green or yellow-green, both surfaces glabrous or with short soft hairs, veins parallel with distinct small transverse veins, texture light and fragile. Odor faint, taste mild. Preferred grade: bluish-green color, large leaves, few petioles, no roots or inflorescences.
Effects of Common Lophatherum Herb (Dan Zhu Ye)
Functions
This product is the dried stems and leaves of the grass family plant Common Lophatherum Herb (Dan Zhu Ye).
Treats febrile disease with thirst, vexation, dark or painful urination, turbid painful dribbling, oral ulcers and tongue sores, swollen painful gums.
Channels entered
Enters the Heart channel, Lung channel, Gallbladder channel, and Stomach channel.
Main indications
Clears Heart fire, relieves vexation and heat, promotes urination. Treats febrile disease with thirst, vexation, dark or painful urination, turbid painful dribbling, oral ulcers and tongue sores, swollen painful gums.
① Compendium of Materia Medica: relieves vexation and heat, promotes urination, clears the Heart.
② Essentials of the Nature of Crude Herbs: dissolves phlegm and stops thirst, eliminates Upper Burner fire, brightens the eyes, promotes urination, treats leukorrhea, reduces fever, disperses hemorrhoidal pus and toxins.
③ Woling Materia Medica: dispels stomach heat.
④ Materia Medica Renewed: clears Heart fire, promotes urination, relieves vexation and thirst, antidote for pediatric pox toxins; external lesions with malignant toxins.
⑤ Handy Prescriptions from Flora: dissolves phlegm, stops thirst. Treats vexation and heat, cough and asthma, hemoptysis, vomiting and retching, pediatric convulsions.
⑥ Classified Herbal Natures: treats cough and asthma, ocular conditions.
⑦ Modern Practical Chinese Medicine: cools and relieves heat, diuretic. Treats febrile disease with thirst, painful or scanty urination, vexation with insomnia, swollen gums, stomatitis.
⑧ Guangxi Materia Medica Records: treats nasal bleeding.
Dosage and administration
Internal use: decoction, 9–15 g.
Precautions and contraindications
Use with caution in patients without real fire or damp-heat; contraindicated in constitutional deficiency with cold.
Formulas using Common Lophatherum Herb (Dan Zhu Ye)
① For treating febrile thirst: Common Lophatherum Herb (Dan Zhu Ye) five sheng, Poria (Fu Ling), Gypsum (Shi Gao) (crushed) each three taels, wheat three sheng, Trichosanthes fruit (Gua Lou) two taels. Boil the five ingredients: first boil the bamboo leaf in two dou of water, take eight sheng, then add the remaining herbs and boil to obtain four sheng, remove dregs and take warm. (Wai Tai Mi Yao formula: Bamboo Leaf Decoction)
② After resolution of typhoid, emaciation and shortness of breath, qi counterflow with retching: bamboo leaf two handfuls, Gypsum (Shi Gao) one sheng, Pinellia (Ban Xia) (washed) half jin, Ginseng (Ren Shen) two taels, Ophiopogon root (Mai Dong) (removed core) one sheng, Licorice root (Gan Cao) (prepared) two taels, Japonica rice half sheng. (Shang Han Lun: Bamboo Leaf and Gypsum Decoction)
③ After cholera relief, vexation, restlessness and thirst, inability to lie still: strongly boil bamboo leaf and drink five or six he (units). (Sheng Ji Zong Lu: Bamboo Leaf Decoction)
④ Pediatric heart wind-heat with mental confusion: Common Lophatherum Herb (Dan Zhu Ye) one handful, Japonica rice one he, Capillaris wormwood (Yin Chen) half tael. Boil the two herbs in two large cups of water to obtain one cup of juice, remove dregs, add rice and make congee to eat. (Sheng Hui Fang: Dan Zhu Ye Congee)
⑤ Postpartum wind-stroke with fever, face flushed red, panting and headache: bamboo leaf one handful, Kudzu root (Ge Gen) three taels, Saposhnikovia root (Fang Feng) one tael, Platycodon root (Jie Geng) and Licorice root (Gan Cao) each one tael, Cinnamon twig (Gui Zhi) one tael, Ginseng (Ren Shen) one tael, Aconite (Fu Zi) (processed) one piece, Jujube (Da Zao) fifteen pieces, Fresh ginger (Sheng Jiang) five taels. Boil the ten herbs in one dou of water to obtain two and a half sheng, divide and take warm in three doses; cover warmly to induce sweating. (Jin Gui Yao Lue: Bamboo Leaf Decoction)
⑥ For various dribbling painful urinary disorders: Common Lophatherum Herb (Dan Zhu Ye), Plantain seed (Che Qian Zi), Jujube (Da Zao), Black bean (Wu Dou) (roasted, shelled), Juncus pith (Deng Xin Cao), Licorice root (Gan Cao) each 1.5 qian. Prepare as one dose: use two cups of water, decoct to seventy percent, remove dregs, take warm at any time. (Qi Xiao Liang Fang: Dan Zhu Ye Decoction)
⑦ For Heart-heat transferring to the small intestine, oral ulcers and painful dribbling: Common Lophatherum Herb (Dan Zhu Ye) 2 qian, Akebia stem (Mu Tong) 1 qian, raw Licorice root (Sheng Gan Cao) 8 fen, Plantain seed (Che Qian Zi) (roasted) 3 qian, Raw Rehmannia (Sheng Di Huang) 6 qian, decoct in water and take. (Yi Li Jian Yi: Dao Chi San)
⑧ Postpartum extreme collapse of blood and qi with sweating: Dan Zhu Ye, decoct into three he of soup, take slightly warm, then take another dose shortly after. (Jing Xiao Chan Bao)
⑨ For eruptive head sores that come and go with red burning pain: burn one jin of bamboo leaf to ash, pound and sieve into fine powder, mix evenly with egg white, apply three to four times daily. (Sheng Hui Fang)
⑩ For summer-heat qi deficiency with vexation: fresh bamboo leaf (Xian Zhu Ye) and Pseudostellaria root (Tai Zi Shen) each 9 g, Hyacinth bean flower (Bian Dou Hua) 6 g, fresh Lotus leaf (He Ye) half leaf, decoct and take. (Anhui Materia Medica)
