Jiu Ping Hua (Jiu Ping Hua)

Names
Little azalea (Xiaodujian), Liangmao azalea (Liangmao dujuan)
Little azalea (Xiaodujian)
Liangmao azalea (Liangmao dujuan)
Cultivation and processing of Azalea (Jiupinghua)
Source
The root of the Ericaceae plant Liangmao azalea (Liangmao dujuan). Collected in summer and autumn. Sliced and sun-dried.
[Morphology]
Evergreen small shrub, 0.5–2 m tall. Branchlets slender and numerous, light brown, densely covered with reddish-brown appressed hairs. Leaves alternate, crowded at branch tips; petiole short, 2–5 mm long, with brown coarse appressed hairs. Leaf blade leathery, oblong, obovate-elliptic or oblong-lanceolate, 1–4.5 cm long, 0.5–2 cm wide, apex acute, shortly acuminate or acuminate, base short cuneate, entire margin, adaxial surface dark green with sparse appressed long hairs, abaxial surface pale green with scattered reddish-brown flattened appressed hairs; midrib slightly prominent on both surfaces, lateral veins and reticulate veins not obvious. Flowers 3–6, borne at branch tips and leaf axils, forming umbel- or raceme-like inflorescences; pedicel 3–6 mm long, with shiny reddish-brown fine hairs; calyx small, 5-lobed or sometimes unlobed, 1–4 mm long, with reddish-brown fine hairs; corolla funnel-shaped, slightly bilaterally symmetrical, light purplish-red, pink, or bright purple, 5-lobed, lobes spreading, the upper three lobes with deep red spots inside; stamens 5, longer than corolla, filaments with fine soft hairs on the lower part, anthers dehiscing by apical pores; pistil 1, ovary 5-loculed, densely covered with reddish-brown long coarse hairs, style slender, glabrous or hairy at the base. Capsule small, ovoid, 4–9 mm long, exterior densely covered with reddish-brown coarse appressed hairs, persistent style, dehiscent into five valves at maturity. Flowering March–May, fruiting June–July.
[Habitat and distribution]
Ecological environment: grows in mountain meadows or hillside scrub at altitudes of 1000–2500 m, under pine forests, in mixed broadleaf forests or under mixed coniferous-broadleaf canopies, frequently found in limestone mountain shrubland.
Resource distribution: distributed in Shaanxi, Sichuan, Guizhou, Yunnan, and other areas.
Properties and flavor
Astringent, cool.
Effects of Azalea (Jiupinghua)
Actions
The root of the Ericaceae plant Liangmao azalea (Liangmao dujuan). Used for common cold, acute and chronic nephritis, pyelonephritis, and pediatric convulsions.
Channels entered
Heart and Bladder meridians
Indications
Clears heat and releases the exterior, promotes diuresis. Used for common cold, acute and chronic nephritis, pyelonephritis, and pediatric convulsions.
Usage and dosage
Oral: decoction, 15–30 g.
