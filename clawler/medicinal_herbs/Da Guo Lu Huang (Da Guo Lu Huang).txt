Da Guo Lu Huang (Da Guo Lu Huang)
Source: https://zhongyibaike.com/wiki/%E5%A4%A7%E8%BF%87%E8%B7%AF%E9%BB%84
Name
Ginger-flower herb; phlegm medicine.
[Source]
It is the whole herb of the Primulaceae (primrose family) plant Da guo lu huang (Dà guòlùhuáng). Harvested in summer, sun-dried or used fresh.
[Morphology]
Annual or perennial herb; stems usually tufted, bent at the nodes and erect, 10–30 cm high; in shady moist habitats lower stems often prostrate with roots at the nodes, upper parts zigzag ascending, up to 60 cm long, simple or many-branched, densely covered with multicellular hairs 1–1.5 mm long; leaves opposite; petiole 2–12 times shorter than the blade, densely covered with soft hairs; the two pairs of leaves at the stem apex are closely spaced and clustered in a whorl-like manner, often 1–2 times larger than lower leaves; leaf blades ovate to ovate-elliptic, 1.5–8 cm long, 8–40 mm wide, apex acute or slightly obtuse with an abrupt tip, base broadly cuneate, both surfaces with rough appressed hairs, midrib somewhat broad and conspicuous beneath; inflorescence terminal, nearly capitate, many-flowered; pedicels 1–7 mm, densely covered with soft hairs; calyx lobed nearly to the base, lobes lanceolate, abaxial surface with soft hairs; corolla yellow, 10–13 mm long, basal connate portion about 3 mm, apex 5-lobed, lobes obovate, with pellucid gland dots; stamens 5, filaments connate at the base forming a tube and free above, anthers ovate-lanceolate, dehiscence edges ciliate; style up to 8 mm, lower part and apex of the ovary hairy; capsule brown, 3.5–4 mm in diameter; flowering May–June, fruiting August–September.
[Habitat and distribution]
Ecological environment: grows under broadleaf forests and on valley stream banks and roadsides at altitudes of 600–2600 m.
Resource distribution: distributed in Jiangxi, Zhejiang, Hubei, Hunan, Guangxi, Guizhou, Sichuan, Yunnan and other areas.
Cultivation and processing of Da guo lu huang (Dà guòlùhuáng)
Nature and flavor
Bland; neutral in nature.
Effects of Da guo lu huang (Dà guòlùhuáng)
Efficacy
It is the whole herb of the Primulaceae (primrose family) plant Ye tou guo lu huang (Yètóu guòlùhuáng). Main indications are wind-heat cough, sore throat, and heat-toxin sores and scabies.
Channels entered
Lung; Stomach channels
Main indications
Disperses wind, clears heat and detoxifies. Transforms phlegm. Indicated for wind-heat cough, sore throat, and heat-toxin sores and scabies.
Dosage and administration
Internal use: decoction, 15–30 g.
External use: appropriate amount, fresh material pounded and applied; or decocted and used as a wash.
