Cultivation and Processing of Calcite (Han Shui Shi)
Source
It is the crystal of the sulfate mineral mirabilite (Mang Xiao).
Taste and Properties
Pungent and salty; cold in nature.
Appearance
Usually occurs as regular massive crystals, often in a rhombic-prismatic form, with edges and angles; colorless or yellowish-white, transparent, slightly translucent or opaque, surface smooth with a glassy luster. Hard in texture, easily broken; fragments are square or rectangular. Odorless, with a mild taste.
Effects of Calcite (Han Shui Shi)
Functions
It is the crystal of the sulfate mineral mirabilite (Mang Xiao).
Main indications: febrile epidemic diseases, accumulated heat with vexation and thirst, vomiting and diarrhea, edema, urinary retention, gum bleeding, erysipelas, and burns.

Indications
Clears heat and reduces fire, opens the orifices, reduces swelling. Treats febrile epidemic diseases, accumulated heat with vexation and thirst, vomiting and diarrhea, edema, urinary retention, gum bleeding, erysipelas, and burns.
① Ben Jing: Indicated for body heat, accumulation of pathogenic qi in the abdomen, skin burning like fire, vexation and fullness, and water accumulation.
② Ben Cao Jing Ji Zhu: Counteracts croton (Ba Dou) poisoning.
③ Bie Lu: Removes epidemic heat, latent heat in the five viscera, stomach heat, vexation and fullness, thirst, edema, and lower abdominal obstruction.
④ Yi Xue Ru Men: Treats pediatric erysipelas; burn to powder, mix with vinegar and apply topically.
⑤ Gang Mu: Treats whitish urine, internal obstruction, cools the blood and reduces fire, stops toothache, strengthens the teeth and clears the eyes.
⑥ Yi Lin Zuan Yao: Eliminates delirious heat; treats great epidemic heat and cholera-like vomiting and diarrhea, vexation and thirst, and damp-heat edema.
⑦ Ben Cao Qiu Zhen: Applied topically for scalds and burns.
⑧ Ben Cao Qiu Yuan: Treats true heat in the heart and kidney.
Dosage and Administration
Internal use: decocted as a decoction, 9–15 g; or used in pills or powders.
External use: grind to a powder and mix or apply as a paste topically.
Precautions and Contraindications
Contraindicated in patients with deficiency-cold of the spleen and stomach.
① Ben Cao Jing Ji Zhu: Avoid use with Di Shu.
② Ben Cao Jing Shu: Not suitable for those with yin deficiency and flourishing fire, coughing up blood, excessive phlegm, tidal fever and steaming bone disorder, or those with spleen and stomach leading to diarrhea.
③ Ben Cao Qiu Zhen: Absolutely contraindicated in deficient persons with floating heat.
Prescriptions using Calcite (Han Shui Shi)
① For accumulated heat in the zang-fu organs and epidemic heat causing vexation and thirst: Condensed Water Stone (Ning Shui Shi),
Gypsum (Shi Gao),
Talc (Hua Shi) each five qian, Licorice (Gan Cao) two qian. Grind to a powder. Each dose one qian, taken with plain water. (Fang Mai Zheng Zong)
② For typhoid with madness, or throwing off clothes and running about, climbing walls and roofs: Calcite (Han Shui Shi),
Coptis (Huang Lian) (pith removed) equal parts. Grind finely; each dose two qian, steep in a strong decoction of Licorice (Gan Cao), let cool and take. (Ben Shi Fang — Que Shi San)
③ To remove heat-induced epilepsy:
Rhubarb (Da Huang),
Dried Ginger (Gan Jiang),
Dragon Bone (Long Gu) four liang each,
Cinnamon Twig (Gui Zhi) three liang,
Licorice (Gan Cao),
Oyster Shell (Mu Li) two liang each,
Calcite (Han Shui Shi),
Talc (Hua Shi),
Halloysite (Chi Shi Zhi),
White Halloysite (Bai Shi Zhi),
Amethyst (Zi Shi Ying),
Gypsum (Shi Gao) six liang each. The above twelve ingredients are pounded and coarsely sifted, placed in a deerskin bag; take a three-finger pinch, boil in three sheng of well water to three boils, take one sheng warm. (Jin Kui Yao Lue — Feng Yin Tang)
④ For wind-heat with irritability, dry mouth, uncontrolled speech, intense body heat and various toxins: Calcite (Han Shui Shi) half a jin (roasted for half a day in a clean pit in the ground, covered with a basin, sealed with wet earth on all sides, retrieved after one night), add powdered Licorice (Gan Cao) and Bamboo Bezoar (Tian Zhu Huang) two liang each, Borneol (Bing Pian) two fen. Make into glutinous rice paste pills the size of a seed, coat with honey water. (Yao Seng Tan Ji Yan Fang — Long Nao Gan Lu Wan)
⑤ For urinary retention in males and females unable to urinate: Calcite (Han Shui Shi) two liang,
Talc (Hua Shi) one liang,
Malva seed (Dong Kui Zi) one he. Make into powder. Boil one dou of water down to five sheng; take one sheng as needed to induce urination. (Yong Lei Qian Fang)
⑥ For pediatric erysipelas with red hot skin: Condensed Water Stone (Ning Shui Shi) five qian, mix with water and pig bile and apply. (Ben Cao Hui Yan)
⑦ For bleeding inside the teeth with draining orifices, frequent spitting of blood: Calcite powder (Han Shui Shi),
Cinnabar (Zhu Sha),
Licorice (Gan Cao),
Camphor (Zhang Nao) in equal parts, finely powdered. Use a small amount dry to plug the bleeding orifices. (Pu Ji Fang)
⑧ For scalds and burns: roast Calcite (Han Shui Shi), grind and apply. (Wei Sheng Yi Jian Fang)
⑨ For alcoholic-induced jaundice, poor appetite, frequent urination pale like water: Calcite (Han Shui Shi), White Halloysite (Bai Shi Zhi),
Trichosanthes fruit (Gua Lou) five fen each,
Dodder seed (Tu Si Zi) (soaked in wine),
Anemarrhena (Zhi Mu),
Cinnamon bark (Gui Xin) three fen each, finely powdered. Each dose five fen seven, take with wheat porridge, three times daily. (Qian Jin Yi Fang — Han Shui Shi San)
⑩ For pediatric erysipelas with red hot skin: Condensed Water Stone (Ning Shui Shi) (powder) five qian, mix with water and pig bile and apply. (Ben Cao Hui Yan)
⑾ For burns: Calcite (Han Shui Shi) 30 g,
Gypsum (Shi Gao) 30 g,
Calamine (Lu Gan Shi) 30 g. Grind the three into a fine powder by dry-frying, add Borneol (Bing Pian) 3 g to make an extremely fine powder. Spray locally onto the wound. (Zhongshan Medical College — Zhong Yao Lin Chang Ying Yong)
⑿ For abscesses: Calcite processed by water-flying, mixed with pig fat rendered in the twelfth lunar month to make an ointment; spread according to ulcer size and cover with thin paper. (Ben Shi Fang — Tai Bai Gao)
⒀ For chronic or recent unstoppable cough and wheeze:
Coltsfoot flower (Kuan Dong Hua) one liang, Calcite (Han Shui Shi),
Pinellia (Ban Xia),
Alum (Ming Fan) two liang each. Grind to a fine powder, cook with Fresh Ginger (Sheng Jiang) juice into a paste and form pills the size of catalpa seeds. Each dose thirty to forty pills, taken at any time with Fresh Ginger (Sheng Jiang) decoction. (Qi Xiao Liang Fang — Bai Yun Huan Fei Wan)
