Cultivation and processing of powdered shell of freshwater mussel
Source
Made from the shells of Unionidae family mollusks such as the dorsal-horn toothless mussel, the corrugated crown mussel, or the triangular-sail mussel. The shells are washed clean, the black skin scraped off, and ground into powder or calcined to ash for use.
Properties and flavor
Salty, cold.
Effects of powdered shell of freshwater mussel
Functions
Made from the shells of Unionidae family mollusks such as the dorsal-horn toothless mussel, the corrugated crown mussel, or the triangular-sail mussel.
Transforms phlegm and reduces accumulations, clears heat and dries dampness. Treats cough due to phlegm-fluid retention, stomach pain, nausea and vomiting, leukorrhea, abscesses and swellings, and damp sores.
Channels entered
Enters the Lung, Liver, and Stomach channels.
Main indications
Transforms phlegm and reduces accumulations, clears heat and dries dampness. Treats cough due to phlegm-fluid retention, stomach pain, nausea and vomiting, leukorrhea, abscesses and swellings, and damp sores.
① Ben Cao Shiyi: Rotten shells powdered and taken internally treat nausea and phlegm-fluid accumulation in the chest.
② Ri Huazi Materia Medica: Treats gan (infantile malnutrition), stops dysentery and vomiting; for abscesses mix with vinegar and apply topically.
③ Compendium of Materia Medica (Bencao Gangmu): Relieves heat and dries dampness, transforms phlegm and reduces accumulation. Stops white turbidity and vaginal discharge, dysentery; removes damp swellings, treats water-related cough, improves the eyes, and treats genital sores, damp sores, and itchy carbuncles.
④ Yilin Zuanyao: Treats stubborn phlegm, stops coughing, clears the heart and preserves the lungs.
Dosage and administration
Internal use: used in pills and powders, 3–6 g.
External use: appropriate amount as a dry coarse powder; or mix into a paste and apply.
