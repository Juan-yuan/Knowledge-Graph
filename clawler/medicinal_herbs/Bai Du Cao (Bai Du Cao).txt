Bai Du Cao (Bai Du Cao)

Ear-leaved Golden-hair Bracken (Er ye jin mao luo jue)
Cultivation and processing of Baiducao (Bai Du Cao)
Source
From the rhizome or whole herb of Ear-leaved Golden-hair Bracken (Er ye jin mao luo jue), a plant of the family Dennstaedtiaceae. Collected year-round or in summer and autumn, washed clean, used fresh or sun-dried.
[Morphology]
Plants 8–22 cm high. Rhizome creeping, together with the bases of the petioles densely covered with rust-red, linear-lanceolate scales and white soft hairs. Leaves nearly clustered; petiole wiry, rigid, 5–10 cm long, chestnut-colored, densely covered from the base upward with long pale-brown soft hairs; leaf blades thick-papery, lanceolate, 5–15 cm long, 1.5–4 cm wide, the apical pinna largest, up to 3 cm long, once-pinnate; pinnae 3–11 pairs, alternate, ovate or oblong-ovate, base cordate, asymmetric, sometimes with small auriculate protrusions, apex obtuse, base with short stalks, margins entire, adaxial surface dark green sparsely covered with soft hairs, abaxial surface densely covered with rust-colored soft hairs. Sori linear, borne along both sides of the lateral veins, hidden among the soft hairs; indusia absent.
[Habitat and distribution]
Ecology: grows on dry rocks on mountain slopes at elevations of 900–2000 m.
Distribution: occurs in Northeast China, North China, Northwest China, Southwest China and in provinces such as Shandong, Henan, Hubei.
Nature and taste
Bitter; cold
Characteristics
Rhizome densely covered with rust-yellow narrow lanceolate long scales. Petioles chestnut-brown, covered with soft hairs up to the rachis. Leaf blades thick-papery, 5–15 cm long, 1.5–4 cm wide, once-pinnate with an odd number of pinnae, pinnae bases deeply cordate, sometimes with 1–2 small auricles, both surfaces with long brown hairs; lateral veins repeatedly forked, veinlets free or occasionally anastomosing near the margin to form narrow reticulate areoles. Sori borne along the lateral veins, covered by soft hairs. Odor faint, taste bitter.
Effects of Baiducao (Bai Du Cao)
Actions
The rhizome or whole herb of Ear-leaved Golden-hair Bracken (Er ye jin mao luo jue). Mainly used for wind-toxin sores and itching, eczema, and vaginal discharge.
Indications
Detoxify, dry dampness and relieve itching. Primarily for wind-toxin sores and itching, eczema, and vaginal discharge.
Usage and dosage
Internal: decoction, rhizome 9–15 g.
External: whole herb as appropriate, decocted for washing.
