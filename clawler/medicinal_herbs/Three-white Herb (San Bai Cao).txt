Three-white Herb (San Bai Cao)

Names
Water Mutong (Shuimu tong) (Additions to the Compendium of Materia Medica), Five-way White (Wulu bai), White Water Chicken (Baishui ji) (Fujian Folk Herbal Medicine), Water-companion Deep-black (Shuibanshenwu), White-faced Aunt (Baimiangu) (Guangxi Veterinary Medicinal Plants), Pond-crossing Lotus (Guotang lian) (Nanning Materia Medica), Three-dot White (Sandian bai), Water-buffalo Grass (Shui niu cao), Water Nine-joint Lotus (Shui jiu jie lian) (Jiangxi Folk Herbal Medicine), White-flowered Lotus (Baihua lian), White-leaf Lotus (Baiye lian) (Jiangxi Folk Herbal Prescription Formulas), One White Two White (Yi bai er bai) (Hunan Materia Medica), Field Sanbai (Tian sanbai), Earth Jade Bamboo (Tu yuzhu), White Yellow Foot (Bai huang jiao) (Mindong Materia Medica), Five-leaf White (Wuye bai), White Orange-morning (Bai jiezhao), White-flower Reflecting-water Lotus (Baihua zhaoshui lian) (Fujian Chinese Medicinal Herbs), Innate-nature Herb (Tianxing cao) (Selected Materials on Chinese Herbal Medicine for Tumor Treatment).
Cultivation and processing of Sanbai herb (Sanbai cao)
Source
Derived from the whole herb of the Sanbai grass plant (family Sanbai grass). Can be harvested in all seasons; wash clean and sun-dry. Collect the above-ground parts from July to September and sun-dry.
Morphology
Perennial herbaceous plant, 30–90 centimeters tall. Rhizome with hair-like small roots. Stem erect, or prostrate at the base, with longitudinal ribs, glabrous. Leaves simple and alternate; petiole 2–3 centimeters long, base clasps the stem; leaf blade ovate or ovate-lanceolate, 6–14 centimeters long and 3–7 centimeters wide, apex acute or gradually acuminate, base cordate and slightly auriculate, margin entire or nearly entire, green, glabrous on both surfaces, with five basal veins; the two or three leaves below the terminal inflorescence often turn white in early summer. Racemose inflorescences borne at the stem apex, opposite the leaves, up to 14 centimeters long; main peduncle and pedicels pubescent; bracts oblanceolate, about 2 millimeters long, with fine marginal hairs; flowers bisexual, apetalous, arising from the base of the bract; stamens six, filaments equal in length to the anthers; pistil single, formed by the fusion of four carpels, ovary globose, four stigmas reflexed outward. Capsules dehisce apically at maturity. Seeds spherical. Flowering period May–August. Fruiting period June–September.
Habitat and distribution
Grows beside ditches, in marshes and other low, wet sites and places near water. Distributed in Hebei, Shandong, Anhui, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Guangdong, Hunan, Hubei, Jiangxi, Sichuan and other provinces.
Properties and flavor
Bitter and pungent, cold.
① Tang Materia Medica: taste sweet and pungent, cold, slightly toxic.
② Fujian Chinese Medicinal Herbs: bitter and pungent, cool.
Characteristics
The rhizome is cylindrical, slightly curved, branched, of variable length; surface gray-brown, rough, with nodes and longitudinal wrinkles, nodal roots present, appearing annulate, internodes about 2 centimeters long; texture hard and brittle, breaks easily, fracture surface off-white and powdery. Stem cylindrical, with four longitudinal grooves, one broader than the others; fracture surface yellow, fibrous, hollow. Leaves simple and alternate, leaf blades ovate or ovate-lanceolate, 4–15 centimeters long and 2–10 centimeters wide; apex gradually acuminate, base cordate, margin entire, with five basal veins; petiole relatively long, with longitudinal wrinkles. Racemose inflorescence at branch tips opposite the leaves, flowers small and brownish. Capsule nearly globose. Slight odor, bland taste.
Actions of Sanbai herb (Sanbai cao)
Efficacy
Derived from the whole herb of the Sanbai grass plant (family Sanbai grass). Chiefly used for edema, beriberi, jaundice, turbid urinary discharges, vaginal discharge, sores and swellings, and detoxification.
Meridians entered
Enters the Lung and Bladder meridians.
Indications
Clears and drains damp-heat, reduces swelling, detoxifies. Treats edema, beriberi, jaundice, turbid urinary discharges, vaginal discharge, sores and swellings, and detoxification.
① Tang Materia Medica: treats edema and beriberi, promotes urination for both small and large amounts, disperses phlegm and resolves stubborn masses, removes accumulations, reduces carbuncles and swellings.
② Additions to Materia Medica: crushed and pressed juice taken causes vomiting and alleviates oppressive heat phlegm in the chest and diaphragm; also used for malaria and childhood abdominal distension.
③ Illustrated Examination of Plant Names and Substance: commonly used for treating tendons and bones and for regulating menstruation in women.
④ Lingnan Collection of Medicinal Plants: treats turbid urinary discharges, promotes urination, clears heat and detoxifies.
⑤ Guangxi Materia Medica: treats leukorrhea in women and pox scabies.
⑥ Materia Medica Commentary: treats hot strangury, deficient strangury, and jaundice.
⑦ Hunan Materia Medica: treats dysentery and snakebite.
Usage and dosage
Internal use: decoction, 9–15 grams; or crush and drink the juice.
External use: use fresh herb as appropriate, crush and apply as a poultice or decoct for washing.
Formulas of Sanbai herb (Sanbai cao)
① For carbuncles and inflammatory swellings: a handful of fresh Sanbai herb leaves, crush into a paste and apply to the affected area, change twice daily. (Fujian Folk Herbal Medicine).
② For xiùqiú wind (a local skin disorder): fresh Sanbai herb, crush and use the juice to wash the affected area. (Zhejiang Tianmu Mountain Medicinal Plants Records).
