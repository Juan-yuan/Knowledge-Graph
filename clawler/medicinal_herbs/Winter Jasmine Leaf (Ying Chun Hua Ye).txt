Winter Jasmine Leaf (Ying Chun Hua Ye)

Cultivation and processing of winter jasmine (Yingchunhua) leaves
Source
Leaves of the Oleaceae plant winter jasmine (Yingchunhua). Collected in summer and autumn, used fresh or sun-dried.
Morphology
Deciduous shrub, erect or prostrate, height 0.3–5 m. Branchlets four-angled, sometimes with narrow wings on the angles. Leaves opposite, ternate compound leaves, basal parts of branchlets often with simple leaves; rachis with narrow wings; petiole 3–10 mm long; leaflets ovate, oblong-ovate or elliptic, narrowly elliptic, rarely obovate, apex acutely or obtusely pointed with a short acumen; base cuneate, margins revolute; terminal leaflet larger, 1–3 cm long, 0.3–1.1 cm wide, sessile or with base prolonged into a short petiolule, lateral leaflets 0.6–2.3 cm long, 0.2–1 cm wide, sessile or with base prolonged into a short petiolule; simple leaves ovate or elliptic, sometimes nearly circular. Flowers solitary in the leaf axils of last year’s branchlets, rarely at the tips of branchlets; bracts leaflike, lanceolate, ovate or elliptic; pedicel 2–3 mm long; calyx green, lobes 5–6, narrow-lanceolate, apex acute; corolla yellow, 2–2.5 cm in diameter, corolla tube 0.8–2 cm long, 3–6 mm wide, gradually expanding upward, lobes 5–6, oblong or elliptic, 0.8–1.3 cm long, 3–6 mm wide, apex acute or obtuse; stamens 2, inserted in the corolla tube; ovary 2-loculed. Flowering April–May.
Habitat and distribution
Ecological environment: grows in hillside scrub.
Resource distribution: found in Shaanxi, Gansu, Sichuan, Yunnan, and Tibet. Cultivated in many places.
Nature and taste
Bitter; cold in nature.
Characteristics
Leaves mostly curled and wrinkled; when leaflets are flattened they are ovate or broadly ovate, 1–3 cm long, apex acutely pointed, margins with short cilia, underside glabrous, gray-green. Odor faintly aromatic, taste slightly bitter and astringent.
Effects of winter jasmine (Yingchunhua) leaves
Therapeutic actions
Leaves of the Oleaceae plant winter jasmine (Yingchunhua). Used for common cold with fever, painful and difficult urination, vulvar itching, carbuncles and malignant sores, traumatic injuries from falls or blows, and bleeding from cuts.
Indications
Clears heat, promotes diuresis and relieves dampness, and detoxifies. Indicated for common cold with fever, painful urinary dripping, vulvar itching, carbuncles and malignant sores, traumatic injuries, and bleeding from cuts.
Dosage and administration
Internal use: decoction, 10–20 g.
External use: appropriate amount, decocted for washing; or crushed and applied as a poultice.
Formulas using winter jasmine (Yingchunhua) leaves
For carbuncles and malignant sores: shade-dry winter jasmine (Yingchunhua) leaves and grind to a powder; take two to three qian with wine by mouth, sweating follows and then cured. (From "Simple and Easy Hygienic Prescriptions")
