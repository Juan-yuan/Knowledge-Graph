Cultivation and processing of gypsum (Shigao)
Source
A sulfate-group gypsum mineral. Generally excavated in winter; after extraction remove adhering soil and miscellaneous stones.
Flavor and properties
Pungent and sweet, cold.
Processing
Raw gypsum (Sheng Shigao): remove impurities and stones, wash off soil, crush into small pieces.
Calcined gypsum (Duan Shigao): take clean gypsum chunks, place in a crucible, calcine in a smokeless furnace until friable, remove, cool, and pulverize.
Appearance
Occurs as long blocky or irregular columnar fibrous crystalline aggregates of varying size. Overall white to off-white. Large blocks have two flat faces, dull and without luster or grain. The material is heavy but loose, easily broken into small pieces; longitudinal fracture shows fibrous texture with a silky luster. Odorless, bland taste. Best quality is large blocks, white in color, loose in texture, fibrous, and free of foreign stone. When burned, the flame is stained pale reddish yellow and it can fuse into white magnetic alkaline beads. When heated to 120°C it loses part of its crystalline water and becomes a white powdery or blocky calcined gypsum.
Effects of gypsum
Actions
A sulfate-group gypsum mineral.
Treats febrile diseases with strong fever that will not remit, vexation and delirium, delirious speech and madness, thirst and dry throat, lung-heat wheezing and panting, summer-heat with spontaneous sweating, stomach-fire causing headache and toothache, excessive febrile toxins causing severe rashes, mouth and tongue sores. Calcined gypsum applied topically promotes tissue generation and restrains sores. Externally treats carbuncles, ulcers that will not close, and scalds and burns.
Channels entered
Enters the Lung meridian and the Stomach meridian.
Indications
Raw use relieves the exterior, clears heat, removes vexation and stops thirst. Calcined and applied externally promotes tissue generation and restrains sores.

Dosage and administration
15–60 g, decoct first.
Contraindications and cautions
Contraindicated in Spleen and Stomach deficiency cold, and in heat from blood deficiency or yin deficiency.
1. Bencao Jing Jizhu: Egg white as an adjuvant. Incompatible with Mangcao and Mamu du gong.
2. Yaoxing Lun: Incompatible with Croton (Badou). Averse to iron.
3. Yixue Qiyuan: "Secret of Treatments" states it can cold the stomach and suppress appetite; should not be used lightly unless the abdomen is extremely hot.
Formulas using gypsum
1. For combined three-yang disease with abdominal fullness and heavy body, difficulty turning over, numbness of mouth, dirty face, delirious speech and urine discharge, sweating causes delirium, purging causes forehead sweating, hands and feet cold; if spontaneous sweating:
Anemarrhena (Zhimu) 6 liang, Gypsum (Shigao) 1 jin (crushed), Licorice (roasted, Gancao) 2 liang, Polished rice (Jingmi) 6 he. Combine four ingredients, use one dou of water, cook rice until hot, strain the soup, take warm one sheng, three times daily. (Shang Han Lun: Baihu Tang)
2. For early warm-disease with floating and forceful pulse, strong body heat; also for early-stage common cold when there is no aversion to cold but there is internal heat: Raw gypsum (Sheng Shigao) 2 liang (pounded fine), raw polished rice (Sheng Jingmi) 2.5 liang. Use three large bowls of water, decoct until rice is soft and hot to yield about two large bowls of clear juice; drink hot as much as possible to induce sweat over the whole body; disease will be cured. If Yangming bowel heat is already in excess, do not drink at once while hot; sip warm drinks slowly to eliminate the heat. (Yixue Zhongzhong Canxi Lu: Shigao Jingmi Tang)
3. For damp-warmth with excessive sweating, delirious speech and thirst: Gypsum (Shigao) and roasted Licorice (Gancao) equal parts. Grind to powder. Each dose two qian, suspend in gruel and swallow. (Shanghan Zongbeng Lun)
4. For Taiyin warm-disease where sweating must not be induced, sweating without exudation causing rash, or excessive sweating causing delirium and rash:
Gypsum (Shigao) 1 liang, Anemarrhena (Zhimu) 4 qian, raw Licorice (Gancao) 3 qian, Scrophularia root (Xuanshen) 3 qian, Rhinoceros horn & turtle shell (Xigui) 2 qian, white polished rice 1 he. Use eight cups of water, boil down to three cups, take three times daily; reboil the residue once for one bedtime dose. (Wenbing Tiaobian: Huanban Tang)
5. After sweating when GuiZhi Tang should not be used and coughing occurs without high fever:
Ephedra (Mahuang) 4 liang (nodes removed), Bitter apricot kernel (Kuxingren) 50 pieces (skins and tips removed), Licorice (Gancao, roasted) 1 liang, Gypsum (Shigao) half jin (crushed, wrapped in cotton). Use seven sheng of water, decoct ephedra until two sheng reduced, remove scum, add other herbs, decoct to three sheng, strain, take warm one sheng. (Shang Han Lun: Mahuang Xingren Gancao Shigao Tang)
6. For phlegm-heat wheeze with phlegm gushing like a spring:
Hanshuishi (Cold-water stone) and Gypsum (Shigao) equal parts; grind to fine powder. Decoction with ginseng (Renshen) soup, take three qian after meals. (Suwen Bingji Baoming Ji: Shuangyu San)
7. For severe heat cough and wheeze that is chronic: Gypsum (Shigao) 2 liang, Licorice (Gancao, roasted) 0.5 liang. Grind to powder. Each dose three qian, mix with fresh well-water and take; mix with raw ginger juice and honey to take. (Puji Fang: Shigao San)
8. For bone-steaming afternoon fever, dry mouth and thirst: Gypsum (Shigao) 6 liang (crushed, wrapped in cotton and calcined), large Wumei (Mume) 20 pieces. Use seven sheng of water, decoct to four sheng, remove residue, add three he of honey, sip slowly. (Waitai)
9. For wind-evil epilepsy with dry mouth and tongue, vexation, headache and sudden feverish confusion: Gypsum (Shigao) half jin, polished rice 1–3 he. Use five large cups of water, decoct gypsum to two large cups, remove gypsum, cook rice into congee, add two scallion white stalks (Congbai) and two he fermented soybean sauce (Douzhi), cook together, when warm take on an empty stomach. Gypsum can be used three times. (Shenghui Fang: Shigao Congee)

Dietary therapy recipes
1. Gypsum damp-resolving congee
Raw gypsum 120 g, Aged tangerine peel (Chenpi) 6 g, Bitter apricot kernel (Kuxingren) 15 g, Coix seed (Yiyiren) 30 g, Chestnut kernels (Limi) 50 g, rock sugar to taste. Decoction: boil the first four ingredients, strain and keep the liquid, add chestnuts and cook into congee, when cooked add rock sugar and simmer briefly until dissolved. Take appropriate amounts.
Function: Clears summer heat and disseminates qi, strengthens the Spleen and resolves dampness.
2. Gypsum and water buffalo horn cake
Ingredients: Gypsum 60 g, Water buffalo horn powder 60 g, Anemarrhena (Zhimu) 10 g, Moutan bark (Mud anpi) 15 g, Indigo leaf (Daqingye) 15 g, wheat flour 200 g, rock sugar to taste.
Preparation: Decoction gypsum, water buffalo horn powder, Anemarrhena, Moutan bark and Indigo leaf for 30 minutes, strain and reserve the liquid, add rock sugar and simmer until dissolved. When cool, combine the liquid with flour and prepare pancakes in the usual way; divide into 2–3 doses to take.
Function: Clears heat and detoxifies, cools blood and resolves spots.
