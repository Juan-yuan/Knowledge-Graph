Cultivation and processing of Barberry root (San Ke Zhen)
Source
This drug is the dried roots of several congeneric plants of the family Berberidaceae, such as Berberis soulieana Schneid., Berberis wilsonae Hemsl., Berberis poiretii Schneid., or Berberis vernae Schneid.
Harvested in the spring and autumn, remove soil and fine roots, sun-dry whole or slice and sun-dry.
Processing
Remove impurities; for material not yet sliced, spray clean water until thoroughly moistened, slice, and dry.
Properties and channels entered
Bitter, cold; toxic. Enters the liver, stomach, and large intestine meridians.
Functions and indications
Clears heat and dries dampness; drains fire and detoxifies. Used for damp-heat dysentery, jaundice, eczema, sore throat and red eyes, purulent ear discharge, and carbuncles, swellings, and sores.
Dosage and administration
Internal use: decoct 9–15 g. Or infuse in alcohol.
External use: appropriate amount, grind to powder and apply topically.
Appearance
This drug is subcylindrical, slightly twisted, with a few branches, 10–15 cm long and 1–3 cm in diameter. The root crown is thick and tapers downward. The bark is gray-brown with fine wrinkles and peels easily. Texture hard, not easily broken; cross-section uneven, fresh yellow; slices nearly round or oblong, slightly showing radiating texture; pith brown-yellow. Slight odor, bitter taste.
Effects of Barberry root (San Ke Zhen)
Efficacy
This drug is the dried roots of several species of the genus Berberis in the Berberidaceae, such as Berberis soulieana, Berberis wilsonae, Berberis poiretii, or Berberis vernae. Used for damp-heat dysentery, jaundice, eczema, sore throat and red eyes, purulent ear discharge, and carbuncles, swellings, and sores.
Precautions
Use with caution in cases of spleen and stomach deficiency with cold.
Formulas containing Barberry root (San Ke Zhen)
1. For bloody dysentery: Barberry root (San Ke Zhen) 5 qian, brown sugar 5 qian. Decoction for oral administration.
2. For jaundice: Barberry stem (San Ke Zhen) 5 qian. Decoction for oral administration.
3. For acute congestive conjunctivitis ("hot eyes"): grind the rhizome/rootstock of Barberry (San Ke Zhen) with water and apply to the eye corners.
4. For knife wounds: grind Barberry root (San Ke Zhen) to a powder and apply to the wound.
5. For traumatic injuries from falls and blows: Barberry root (San Ke Zhen) 1 liang. Soak in alcohol, take internally and apply externally. (Prescription selected from Guizhou Herbal Medicine)
