Cultivation and Preparation of Cicada Flower (Jinchanhua)
Source
It consists of the conidiophore clusters of the Clavicipitaceae fungus Chanbangshubaojun (Chanbangshubaojun), the stromata of Large Cicada Herb (Dachancao) and the insect bodies they parasitize. From June to August, they are dug out from the soil, cleaned of dirt, and sun-dried.
[Original morphology]
1. Chanbangshubaojun fungus: conidiophore clusters grow in tufts, arise from the anterior end of the host, white when fresh, 1.5–6 cm high; stalks branched or unbranched, 0.1–0.2 cm in diameter, sometimes connected at the base, branched at the apex and covered with a powdery layer of conidia. Conidia oblong-ovoid, slightly acute at both ends, (6–9) μm × (2–2.5) μm, often contain two oil globules, hyaline and colorless.
2. Large Cicada Herb (Dachancao): insect body long-ellipsoid, slightly curved, about 3 cm long, 1–1.4 cm in diameter, resembling a cicada exuviae. The head of the insect bears 1–2 rod-shaped stromata, linear or curled, branched or unbranched, 3–7 cm long, 3–4 mm in diameter, dark brown, apex slightly swollen, surface with numerous small punctate protrusions.
[Ecological environment]
Grows on cicada larvae.
[Resource distribution]
1. Distributed in Anhui, Zhejiang, Fujian, Guangdong, Sichuan, Yunnan and other areas.
2. Distributed in Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Fujian, Sichuan, Yunnan and other areas.
Processing
Take the crude drug, remove impurities, wash clean, dry, and sieve off ash and debris.
[Nature, Taste and Channels Entered]
Sweet and salty, cold. Enters the Lung and Liver channels.
[Functions and Indications]
Dispel wind and clear heat, promote eruption of rashes, extinguish wind and stop convulsions, brighten the eyes and reduce ocular opacities. Used for externally-contracted wind-heat presenting with fever, dizziness, sore throat; early-stage measles with incomplete eruption; pediatric convulsions, night crying; red, swollen and painful eyes; and opacity of the ocular membrane obstructing vision.
[Dosage and Administration]
Internal use: decoction, 3–9 g.
Description
(1) Chanbangshubaojun fungus
This product consists of the insect body and the conidiophore clusters growing from its head. The insect body is long-ellipsoid, slightly curved, about 3 cm long, 1–4 cm in diameter, surface yellow-brown, mostly covered with gray mycelium, head bearing dense conidiophore clusters. Conidiophore clusters are branched or unbranched, 1.6–6 cm long, divided into a bulbous portion and a stalk; the bulbous portion is long-ellipsoid, ovoid or fusiform, 5–8 mm long, 2–3 mm in diameter, folded and powdery in appearance; the stalk is 1–2 mm in diameter, brown to dark brown. Brittle and easily broken; the insect body is filled with white or off-white soft material. Slight fragrance, mild taste.
(2) Large Cicada Herb (Dachancao)
This product consists of the insect body and the stromata growing from its anterior end. There are 1–2 stromata, branched or unbranched, 3–7 cm long, brown; the head is swollen and tapers toward the tip, 4–6 mm long, 6.5–7 mm in diameter, the surface showing small dots (ostiole openings where perithecia protrude), the stalk 4–5 mm in diameter. The insect body is white, its interior filled with white mycelium. Brittle and easily broken. Slight odor, mild taste.
Effects of Cicada Flower (Jinchanhua)
Efficacy
This product is the dried conidiophore clusters of the Clavicipitaceae fungus Chanbangshubaojun or the dried stromata of Large Cicada Herb (Dachancao) together with the dried parasitized insect bodies. Used for externally-contracted wind-heat: fever, dizziness, sore throat, early-stage measles with incomplete eruption, pediatric convulsions, night crying, red, swollen, painful eyes, and ocular membrane opacity obstructing vision.
