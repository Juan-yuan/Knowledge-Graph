Cultivation and Processing of Hibiscus syriacus Flower (Mùjǐn huā)
Source
This item is the dried flowers of the Malvaceae plant Hibiscus syriacus that are not fully open.
Processing
Hibiscus syriacus Flower: remove stems and other impurities from the crude drug and sieve off dust.
Honey-fried Hibiscus syriacus Flower: take Hibiscus flowers and fry according to the honey-frying method until the honey is fully absorbed. For every 100 kg of Hibiscus flowers, use 40 kg of refined honey.
Characters
1. Hibiscus syriacus Flower
The product is wrinkled, ovate or irregularly cylindrical, often with short flower stalks. The whole is pubescent, length 1.5–3.5 cm, width 1–2 cm. Bracts 6–7, linear. Calyx bell-shaped, gray-yellow-green, apex 5-lobed, lobes triangular. Petals are white, yellowish-white or light brownish-yellow; single petals number 5 or double petals exceed 10. Stamens are numerous, filaments united into a tube. Slight fragrance, bland taste.
2. Honey-fried Hibiscus syriacus Flower
Pale yellow-brown to yellow-brown, moist, has a honey aroma, slightly sweet in taste.
Properties and Meridian Tropism
Sweet, bland, cool. Enters the Spleen and Lung channels.
Functions and Indications
Clears heat and drains dampness, cools the blood and detoxifies. Indicated for intestinal-wind bloody diarrhea, dysentery with red or white stools, bleeding hemorrhoids, lung-heat cough, cough with blood, leukorrhea, sores and carbuncles, and burns.
Dosage and Administration
For internal use, decocted, 3–9 g.
For external use, an appropriate amount of fresh material, crushed and applied to the affected area.
Effects of Hibiscus syriacus Flower (Mùjǐn huā)
Efficacy
This item is the dried, not fully opened flowers of the Malvaceae plant Hibiscus syriacus. Used for intestinal-wind bloody diarrhea, dysentery with red or white stools, bleeding hemorrhoids, lung-heat cough, cough with blood, leukorrhea, sores and carbuncles, and burns.
Prescriptions Using Hibiscus syriacus Flower (Mùjǐn huā)
1. For dysenteric diarrhea with clenched mouth: remove the calyxes from red Hibiscus flowers, shade-dry and grind to powder; first boil two flatbread cakes, dip them in the powder and eat. (Jìjí Xiānfāng)
2. For dysentery with red or white stools: one liang of Hibiscus flowers (half for children), decoct in water, mix with three fen of white honey and take. Use red flowers for bloody dysentery, white flowers for white dysentery; avoid sour and cold foods. (Yúnnán Zhōngyī Yànfāng)
3. For vomiting blood, lower-body bleeding, and dysentery with red or white stools: 9 to 13 Hibiscus flowers. Add boiling water and rock sugar as appropriate, steep for half an hour, take before meals, twice daily. (Fújiàn Mínjiān Cǎoyào)
4. For wind-phlegm obstructing and causing reversal: sun-dry Hibiscus flowers, roast and grind; take one or two spoonfuls per dose, take with boiling water on an empty stomach; white flowers are especially good. (Jiǎnbiàn Dānfāng)
5. For nausea and gastric reversal: thousand-petalled white Hibiscus flowers, shade-dry and powder; mix with aged rice soup and give three to five sips; if there is no change, then adjust and take rice gruel. (Xiùzhēn Fāng — Jǐnhuā sǎn)
6. For female leukorrhea: two qian of Hibiscus flowers, powdered, mixed with human breast milk, spread on rice and steam until cooked, then eat. (Diānnán Běncǎo)
7. For treatment of sores and carbuncles: appropriate amount of fresh Hibiscus flowers, a little sweet rice wine, mash and apply externally. (Jiāngxī Cǎoyào)
