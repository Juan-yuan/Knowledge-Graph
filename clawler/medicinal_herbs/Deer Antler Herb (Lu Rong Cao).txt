Cultivation and Processing of Lu Rong Cao (Lu Rong Cao)
Source
The whole herb of the Scrophulariaceae plant Monochasma savatieri (hairy Lu Rong Cao; Mian Mao Lu Rong Cao).
Morphology
Biennial herb, 15–30 cm tall, whole plant densely covered with silvery-white tomentose hairs. Stems clustered, slender and rigid. Leaves dense, opposite or in whorls of three, sometimes alternate; leaf blades narrow-lanceolate, 1–3 cm long, 1–2 mm wide, entire margin, apex gradually acuminate, base narrow and sessile. Inflorescences with glandular hairs, bracts leaflike, flowers solitary in bract axils; calyx tube nearly as long as the lobes, with 10 ribs, lobes linear; corolla bilabiate, 2–2.5 cm long, longer than the calyx, pale red, throat slightly inflated, upper lip helmet-shaped and curved, 2-lobed, lower lip longer than the upper lip, middle lobe relatively long, throat with two grooves; stamens 4, didynamous, exserted; ovary incompletely two-loculed, with a central axial placenta, ovules numerous. Capsule enclosed within the calyx, oblong, apex sharp, with four longitudinal grooves, dehiscing along one side at maturity. Seeds numerous, flattened. Flowering April–May.
Habitat and Distribution
Grows on rocks or in dry areas. Distributed in Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Fujian, Jiangxi, Hunan and other regions.
Taste and Properties
Slightly bitter, astringent, neutral in nature.
Functions and Indications
Clears heat and detoxifies, cools the blood and stops bleeding. Used for feverish vexation in the chest from colds, cough, hematemesis, dysentery with blood, bloody stools, irregular menstruation, rheumatic bone pain, toothache, and mastitis.
Usage and Dosage
Internal use: decoction, 9–15 g;
External use: appropriate amount, decocted for washing or pounded and applied as a poultice.
Appearance
This product appears as gray-white segmented pieces, the whole surface densely covered with white tomentose hairs. Stem segments cylindrical, slender and rigid, some showing leaf scars from opposite, alternate or whorled leaves. Most leaves are broken; intact leaf blades narrowly lanceolate, 0.6–3 cm long, 1–3 mm wide, entire margin, sessile, upper surface villous, sparser on the lower surface. Capsules oblong, enclosed in the hairy persistent calyx, apex sharp, with four longitudinal grooves, splitting along one side at maturity. Seeds numerous, small, yellow, ovoid, flattened. Odor slight, taste slightly bitter and astringent.
Effects of Lu Rong Cao
Efficacy
This product is the dried aerial part of the Scrophulariaceae plant Monochasma savatieri. Used for feverish vexation in the chest from colds, cough, hematemesis, dysentery with blood, bloody stools, irregular menstruation, rheumatic bone pain, toothache, and mastitis.
