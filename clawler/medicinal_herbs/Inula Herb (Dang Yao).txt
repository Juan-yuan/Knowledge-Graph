Cultivation and Processing of Swertiae Herba (Dang Yao)
Source
This product is the dried whole herb of the Gentianaceae plant Bristly Swertia SzLertia pseudochinensis Hara. Collected in the summer and autumn seasons, impurities are removed, and it is sun-dried.
Processing
Remove impurities, wash quickly, moisten thoroughly, cut into sections, and dry.
Properties and Meridian Entry
Bitter, cold. Enters the liver, stomach, and large intestine meridians.
Functions and Indications
Clears damp-heat and strengthens the stomach. Used for damp-heat jaundice, hypochondriac pain, dysenteric abdominal pain, and loss of appetite.
Usage and Dosage
Internal use: decoct, 6–12 g, reduce appropriately for children; or grind into powder and take with water.
External use: appropriate amount, crush and apply as a poultice; or apply the expressed juice externally.
Description
This product is 10–40 cm long. Roots conical, 2–7 cm long, yellow or yellow-brown, fracture surface whitish. Stem quadrangular-columnar, often with narrow wings, many-branched, diameter 1–2.5 mm; yellow-green or yellow-brown with purple, nodes slightly swollen; brittle, breaks easily, cross-section hollow. Leaves opposite, sessile; entire leaves when spread flat are strap-shaped-spathulate, 2–4 cm long, 0.3–0.9 cm wide, apex gradually acuminate, base narrow, margin entire. Inflorescences are paniculate-umbellate, terminal or axillary. Calyx deeply 5-lobed, lobes linear. Corolla pale blue-purple or dull yellow, deeply 5-lobed, with two glands at the inner base of each lobe, glands surrounded by long hairs. Capsule elliptic. Slight odor, bitter taste.
