Prescription of Guizhi Gancao Decoction (Guizhi Gancao Tang)
Source
Treatise on Cold Damage (Shanghan Lun)
Composition
Cinnamon Twig (Guizhi) 12 g, Prepared Licorice (Zhi Gancao) 6 g
Efficacy
Tonify Heart Qi, warm Heart Yang
Explanation
Excessive sweating injures the Heart Yang; when the Heart's central Yang Qi is damaged it causes palpitations below the heart and a desire to press and restrain, hence clasping the hands over the chest. In the formula Cinnamon Twig (Guizhi) enters the Heart, its acrid warm nature assists Yang; Prepared Licorice (Zhi Gancao) is sweet and warm and benefits Qi, further aiding the restoration of the Heart's Yang Qi. The two herbs combined, with acrid and sweet properties, transform and restore Yang; as Yang recovers and Yin is aided, the Heart is calmed.
Efficacy of Guizhi Gancao Decoction (Guizhi Gancao Tang)
Indications
For excessive sweating where the person clasps their hands over the chest, has palpitations below the heart, and desires to press the chest.
Prescription and Administration
Decoction in water, taken warm in three divided doses (Adult usual dosage: 10 doses)
