Prescription of Guizhi Jia Fuzi Decoction (Guizhi Jia Fuzi Tang)
Source
Treatise on Cold Damage (Shang Han Lun).
Composition
Cinnamon Twig (Guizhi) 9 g (peeled) Peony (Shao Yao) 9 g Licorice (Gan Cao) 9 g (honey-fried) Fresh Ginger (Sheng Jiang) 9 g (sliced) Jujube (Da Zao) 3 pieces (broken) Aconite (Fuzi) 6 g (processed)
Actions of Guizhi Jia Fuzi Decoction (Guizhi Jia Fuzi Tang)
Indications
Harmonize the Ying and Wei, secure the exterior and support Yang. Indicated for Taiyang-stage disorder in which sweating after sweating is excessive causing unceasing perspiration, aversion to wind, scanty and obstructed urination, slight stiffness of the four limbs with difficulty flexing and extending. Used to treat exterior deficiency with leakage of sweat, stubborn night sweats, sinus bradycardia, collapse in children from deficiency, hepatitis B, etc.
Usage
Combine the six ingredients, add 700 milliliters of water, decoct down to 300 milliliters, strain off dregs; each dose 100 milliliters, taken warm. For recuperation, follow the method of Guizhi Decoction (Guizhi Tang).
