Cinnamon Twig Decoction (without Peony) with Aconite (Gui Zhi Qu Shao Yao Jia Fu Zi Tang)
Source: https://zhongyibaike.com/wiki/%E6%A1%82%E6%9E%9D%E5%8E%BB%E8%8A%8D%E8%8D%AF%E5%8A%A0%E9%99%84%E5%AD%90%E6%B1%A4
Prescription of Guizhi Decoction without Paeonia plus Aconite (Guizhi qu Shaoyao jia Fuzi Tang)
Source
Treatise on Cold Damage (Shang Han Lun).
Ingredients
Cinnamon Twig (Guizhi) 9 g (peeled) Licorice (Gancao) 6 g (honey-fried) Jujube (Dazao) 12 pieces (split) Fresh Ginger (Shengjiang) 9 g (cut) Prepared Aconite (Fuzi) 5 g (processed)
Actions of Guizhi Decoction without Paeonia plus Aconite (Guizhi qu Shaoyao jia Fuzi Tang)
Indications
Releases the exterior and dispels wind, warms and tonifies yang qi and generates body fluids. Used for Taiyang disease when, after mistaken application of purgative methods, the pulse is rapid, the chest is full, and there is slight aversion to cold.
Administration
Use the above five ingredients with 700 milliliters of water, decoct to obtain 300 milliliters, strain off dregs, take 100 milliliters warm. For recuperation, follow the method of Guizhi Decoction (Guizhi Tang).
