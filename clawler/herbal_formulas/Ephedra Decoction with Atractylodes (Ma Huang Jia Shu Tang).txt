Prescription of Ephedra Decoction with Atractylodes (Ma Huang Jia Shu Tang)
Source
Essential Prescriptions of the Golden Cabinet (Jin Kui Yao Lue), juan shang
Composition
Original Ephedra Decoction (Ma Huang Tang) plus Bai Zhu (Atractylodes macrocephala) 12g
Efficacy of Ephedra Decoction with Atractylodes (Ma Huang Jia Shu Tang)
Actions
Induces sweating and releases the exterior; disperses cold and expels dampness
Indications
Wind-cold-damp painful obstruction, generalized body vexation and pain, absence of sweating, etc. (i.e., exterior excess pattern of wind-cold entrapping dampness)
Note
The Bai Zhu (Atractylodes macrocephala) in this formula may be substituted with Cang Zhu (Atractylodes lancea). During the Eastern Han period, Cang Zhu and Bai Zhu were not distinguished.
