Prescription of Kudzu Decoction plus Pinellia (Ge Gen Jia Ban Xia Tang)
Source
Treatise on Cold Damage (Shang Han Lun)
Ingredients
Kudzu root (Ge Gen) 12 g Ephedra (Ma Huang) 9 g (nodes removed) Licorice root (Gan Cao) 6 g (honey-fried) Peony root (Shao Yao) 6 g Cinnamon twig (Gui Zhi) 6 g (peeled) Fresh ginger (Sheng Jiang) 6 g (sliced) Pinellia tuber (Ban Xia) 9 g (washed) Chinese jujube (Da Zao) 12 pieces (split)
Therapeutic effects of Kudzu Decoction plus Pinellia (Ge Gen Jia Ban Xia Tang)
Functions
Induces sweating to release the exterior, relaxes the muscles, and stops vomiting.
Indications
Exterior contraction of wind-cold, headache, rigidity and stiffness of the nape and back, absence of sweating, no thirst, nausea and counterflow vomiting, white tongue coating, and a floating pulse.
Administration
Take the above eight herbs with 1 L of water; first decoct the kudzu root and ephedra, reducing to 800 mL, skim off the white froth, add the rest of the herbs, decoct down to 300 mL, strain off the dregs, take 100 mL warm, cover and wait until slight sweating appears.
