Prescription of Gualou Guizhi Decoction (Gualou Guizhi Tang)
Ingredients
Trichosanthes root 6 g (gualou gen) Cinnamon twig 9 g (guizhi) Peony root 9 g (shaoyao) Licorice root 6 g (gancao) Fresh ginger 9 g (shengjiang) Jujube 12 pieces (dazao)
Actions of Gualou Guizhi Decoction (Gualou Guizhi Tang)
Indications
Releases the exterior and disperses pathogens, generates fluids and relaxes the sinews. Used for externally contracted wind-cold with fever and aversion to wind, headache with sweating, body rigidity with slight convulsions, and a pulse that is deep, slow, and forceful.
Administration
Take the six ingredients above with 900 ml of water, boil down to 300 ml, divide into three doses and take warm, to produce slight sweating. If no sweat appears, after a short time sip hot congee to induce it.
Formula Discussion
This formula is essentially Guizhi Tang from the Treatise on Cold Damage (Shanghan Lun) with added Trichosanthes root (gualou gen); the pattern treated is the soft spasm (rou jing) among spasm disorders. It arises from an exterior pathogenic factor obstructing the exterior and the meridians, causing the channels to be constrained and not relaxed; furthermore, exterior deficiency with sweating prevents the body fluids from moistening, producing the condition. In the formula Guizhi Tang externally releases wind-cold, and the addition of Trichosanthes root, which is sweet and cold, moistens dryness, restores the fluids, and is also effective at unblocking the meridians. Combined, the formula achieves the dual effects of releasing the exterior and generating fluids: the exterior signs are resolved, the fluids circulate, the meridians are moistened, and the spasm will then resolve on its own.
